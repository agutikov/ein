## What it is

Today a fact reads by its reason, its `:source` or its rule's `:why`, and where it has none it reads as written.
A fact's own sentence, *Ann owns a dog* for `(owns Ann Dog)`, is what the declaration's `:why` once gave and
nothing gives now. This phase gives every fact a sentence.

## Open, for its planning

Nothing here is ruled; each is a question the phase's first stage puts, on real programs.

1. **Where a relation's sentence for its facts is written**, now that the declaration's `:why` is the
   declaration's own statement (Q-M1f.58): a second text on the declaration, a form of its own, a fact a rule can
   read, or that decision revised.
2. **A relation nobody declares**, and a relation declared more than once or at several counts, each of which the
   language allows.
3. **Every kind of fact**: a fact that holds a fact, a stored negation, a fact under `open`, a declaration, an
   activator, a fact of the standard library's own relations.
4. **A fact's sentence beside its reason.** A line of the trace gives the reason today. Which a surface shows where,
   and whether both.
5. **Which surfaces**: the trace, the drawing's labels, the read-out's listings of a state, a query's answers; and
   which stay Ein.
6. **What holds it**: that no fact a surface shows a reader is left as an s-expression, counted over the corpus.
