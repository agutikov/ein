# Q-M2.22 — Is a membership no sentence states a faithfulness event, or the ontology the formalization searches for?

**Status:** open

**Raised 2026-11-26 by the re-plan of P2.2 to the user's steps**, which made S2.2.0f
[the user's notes on the whole phase](../../README.md#formalization-in-steps-the-users-notes-on-the-whole-phase)
(the phase README's defaults 25 and 40; [S2.2.1](../../s2.2.1_contract.md)'s T2.2.1.2).

**The question.** Step 1 writes, beside the facts, *"part of additional required facts if needed (like instances,
types, is-a, whatever else)"*, which the user counts in the partial ontology, *"expected to change in next step"*;
and *"formalization is search of ontology and theory for given facts"*. The contract's table of categories, written
before the steps, makes a membership fact **source-derived** where it quotes the sentence that names the thing, and
otherwise a **generated assumption**, the one category that is a faithfulness event the moment it appears (the
phase README's defaults 3 and 25; [S2.4.4](../../../p2.4_loop/s2.4.4_faithfulness.md)'s `generated_assumption`).
A membership of a thing a sentence names can quote it, and is source-derived under any reading. The question is
the membership **no sentence names**: a kind and its place, `(type Nationality Attribute)`, of which
[`examples/zebra.ein`](../../../../../examples/zebra.ein) writes seven; or a thing the text implies and never
names, the houses of a puzzle that speaks of *the first house* and *the middle house*. Is such a fact an event
against the program, or the ontology the formalization searches for, a note at most?

**The default taken**: the contract's table as it stands, an event. The first draft of the steps read the other way
(S2.2.0f's T2.2.0f.5: step 1's *"memberships auxiliary"*), and the re-plan did not take it, because a membership
can constrain the answer: a kind given one member more, or an object put in another kind, changes what a selected
theory (`std.slots`, `std.bijection`) owes and refutes, with no sentence behind the change.

**What the default costs.** Every formalization of an attribute puzzle writes kinds no sentence names, so under the
mechanical judge alone every one carries events, and *faithful* (no event) separates nothing on that family until
the canonical judge (b) or the judged sample (c) of [S2.4.4](../../../p2.4_loop/s2.4.4_faithfulness.md) reads it.
It also prices the search the user describes: a step 2 that completes the ontology is counted as inventing.

**The alternatives.**

1. **An event, as the table has it** (the default).
2. **A note, `generated_membership`, a row of the program's frame**: never an event, like a declaration; the
   canonical judge finds the membership that changes the answer, by the comparison of answers it already makes for
   a rule, and the judged sample where there is no canonical program.
3. **An event only where it changes the answer**: the canonical judge's *redundant* or *invented* applied to each
   membership no sentence names; where there is no canonical program, the judged sample decides, and the mechanical
   judge notes it.

**What bears on it.** [Q-M2.3](../../../open_questions/answer/Q-M2.3-what-is-the-unit-of-faithfulness-without-a-gold-theory.md),
the unit of faithfulness, which S2.4.4 decides: whichever answer is taken here is a row of the contract's table that
S2.4.4 reads. The steps' fourth design question (a later step may rename a membership or a kind) holds under any of
the three: what step 2 changes of step 1's ontology is recorded either way.
