# P2.2 — the evaluation of Ein: the phase's entries

What the phase finds of Ein, for [the milestone's evaluation](../../evaluation/README.md), kept as it keeps its own:
one file an entry, `<topic>/E-<n>-<slug>.md`, under the milestone's topics, its id the next free of the milestone's one
sequence of `E-n`, which [the milestone's evaluation](../../evaluation/README.md) names.

**At the phase's close** what is open, and every strength, moves to
[the milestone's `evaluation/`](../../evaluation/README.md), under the same topic; what is closed stays in the
phase's folder.

**The phase closed 2026-11-27, and all twenty-four entries moved**: none was closed in the phase, so every one is
open or a strength, and each is now the milestone's, under its topic, with the stage or the question that owns it
since. The index below is kept as the map of what the phase filed; its links are the milestone's files. E-128, on
what a library says of its rules, was filed at the close, in the milestone's folder.
