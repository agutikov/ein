**Research plan:** [`EinAf.md` § Stage E](../EinAf.md#stage-e--build-the-iterative-formalization-loop) —

## Context

Q8's working answer: a sentence the formalizer cannot resolve is emitted as
**both readings**, each a hypothesis, and the kernel prunes the one that
dies. The research plan's loop handles the same sentence differently —
commit one way, let the verdict and the feedback (F4 names the sentence)
send it back — and the two are *strategies for the same sentence*, which
[P2.6](../p2.6_ablations/README.md) compares. This stage makes the old one
emittable, on two facts the old plan did not have:

1. **There is no `hypothesis-parse` form.** The old S2.1.2 was to reserve
   the keyword and never ran.
2. **`hrule` is already the kernel's choice-point form.** It has *the same
   shape as `rule`* ([`01_grammar.md`](../../../docs/kernel/ir/03-ein-lang/01_grammar.md) § Rules)
   and its conclusions are the *candidates* the commitment lattice
   enumerates — layer 1 is their singletons
   ([design/07](../../../docs/history/m1a_rust/design/07_search_layer.md) § 1;
   *hrule presence is the switch* between the hrule-driven and the blind
   generator). `zebra2.ein` declares one:

   ```lisp
   (hrule guess (?R ?T1 ?T2)
     :match  (and (is-a ?a ?T1) (is-a ?b ?T2))
     :assert (?R ?a ?b)
     :why    "guess: ({?R} {?a} {?b})?")
   ```

So "sentence *S* reads as *A* or *B*" needs no new form. It is an `hrule`
whose `:assert` yields *A* and *B* as candidates, plus a mutual exclusion
— the lattice commits to *subsets* of candidates, and `{A, B}` must die:

```lisp
;; sentence 7: "The man who smokes Chesterfields lives next to the fox owner"
(hrule read-7 ()
  :match  (or (next-to-left  ?h1 ?h2) (next-to-right ?h1 ?h2))   ; shape only — the stage fixes the pattern
  :assert (reading-7 ?h1 ?h2) …)
(rule read-7-exclusive ()
  :match  (and (reading-7-left ?x ?y) (reading-7-right ?x ?y))
  :assert (false))
```

**What the kernel cannot say is "at least one of them"** — a lower bound,
and that is exactly [M1d](../../m1d_satisfiability/README.md)'s finding:
existence is available in *refutation* form only (`(and (not A) (not B))
⇒ (false)`, where the `(not …)` facts come from the singleton `(not h)`
writeback when a candidate dies), never as an obligation. For two readings
the refutation form is enough — the search tries `{A}` and `{B}` as
singletons and the `(false)` kills `{}` once both are excluded — and the
stage writes it that way, with the note that a *third* reading is the
point where an obligation would be the honest encoding and M1d's P1d.2 is
where it arrives.

The surviving reading is recoverable: the `:why` of the `hrule` names it,
the trace's *Refuted hypotheses* section carries the reductio of the other,
and `--events`' `nogood` / `writeback` lines say when it died.

## Acceptance

- `strategy = alternatives` runs end to end on a fixture whose one
  sentence has two readings: both are emitted, one dies, the verdict is
  `unique`, and the transcript names the surviving reading from the trace.
- On the canonical Zebra text the flagging heuristic emits **no**
  alternatives (the old acceptance criterion, kept).
- The encoding uses `hrule` and a `(false)` rule only — no new form, no
  kernel change; if a macro is written it is a `std.macro`-style pattern
  over those two and lives with the harness's prompts, not in `stdlib/`.
- Q8 is marked decided beside its entry: *a strategy, not a default*.

## Tasks

### Task T2.4.5.1 — The encoding and the macro

Fix the pattern above on two fixtures (a `next-to` reading, an `or`
sentence), write it as a template the facts pass can instantiate, and test
that the kernel's verdict on each is the verdict the hand encoding gets.

### Task T2.4.5.2 — Emission

The old heuristic — a sentence containing *or*, *either … or*, *maybe*, a
trailing question mark — sets a flag on that sentence's facts-pass prompt:
*if this sentence has more than one plausible reading, emit each as an
alternative*; the grammar of [S2.2.3](../p2.2_formalizer/s2.2.3_gbnf.md)
gains the alternatives shape **under this strategy only**. The B2 category
of an alternative is *source-derived*, with both readings quoting the
sentence.

### Task T2.4.5.3 — The strategy in the machine

`strategy = alternatives` in [S2.4.2](s2.4.2_repair.md)'s config: GENERATE
emits alternatives where flagged; the REPAIR edge behaves as `repair`. One
arm of [S2.6.4](../p2.6_ablations/s2.6.4_representation_ablations.md)'s
comparison — pre-registered reading: it wins only if it reaches the same
verdict in fewer iterations *and* with no higher hallucination rate.

## Notes

- The old plan put parse choices at "hypothesis level 0" below the
  puzzle's hypotheses. The lattice has no levels, only cardinality; a
  reading and a puzzle guess are both candidates, and the only thing that
  distinguishes them is the `hrule` that produced them, which the trace
  names. That is sufficient, and simpler than what was planned.
