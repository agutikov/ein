## Context

*A research result is meaningless without credible alternatives.* The research plan names the alternatives that
would explain a positive result away: the model already knew the answer (B0); reasoning in text would have found
it (B1); formalizing once was enough (B2); the kernel is a retry trigger and nothing more (B3); one word of
verdict is all the information that matters (B4). B5 is the claim. The user's ruling adds a seventh
([Q-M2.13](../open_questions/einaf/Q-M2.13-who-calls-the-kernel.md)): Ein offered to the model as a function it may
call, B6, which Stage D invites (*where possible add another … structured reasoning approach*).

The arms are [the frame's](../README.md#the-arms). As configs:

| | receives | writes | thinking | what holds the output | the answer is |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **B0** | the text, the instance's answer line | an answer | off | nothing | its message |
| **B1** | the same | a chain of thought, then an answer; then *reconsider* turns to the budget | on, or an instructed chain with the mode off ([T2.5.2.2](#task-t2522--b0-b1-and-b6)) | nothing | the message of the last turn that ended within the budget |
| **B2** | the contract of [S2.2.1](../p2.2_formalizer/s2.2.1_contract.md), the text | one program | off | the harness and the grammar | what the program prints, or none |
| **B3** | B2, then the filter at **F0**: *try again* | a program an iteration | off | the same | the same |
| **B4** | B2, then the filter at **F2**: the verdict | a program an iteration | off | the same | the same |
| **B5** | B2, then the filter at **F8**: everything the kernel found | a program an iteration | off | the same | the same |
| **B6** | the text, the answer line, what B5's prompt says of Ein, and Ein declared as a function with a recommendation to use it | text, and a program where it chooses to call | as B1 | nothing: a parse error is what the tool returns | its last message; whether that equals what its last run printed is recorded |

Held across the arms: the model's file, the sampler chain but for the grammar's place in it
([T2.5.2.3](#task-t2523--the-budget-and-the-matching-rule)), the seeds, the split, the kernel's commit, config
and bounds. Held across B3 to B5, so that the level is the only variable: the
strategy is **repair** (*regenerate* is a row of [P2.6](../p2.6_ablations/README.md)), the termination policy is
[S2.4.1](../p2.4_loop/s2.4.1_state_machine.md)'s with the same budgets, and the questions asked of the kernel are
the same. At F0 the kernel still decides *whether* the loop goes on (a pass stops it); it says nothing of *why*.
That is the point of B3, and why B3 is a loop and not a fixed number of retries.

**The comparison that isolates Ein is not the model against Ein**: B5 spends more than B0 and would win a naive
comparison by spending. It is *same model, similar budget, different information*
([T2.5.2.3](#task-t2523--the-budget-and-the-matching-rule)), and the first reading of the table is B5 and B6
against B1: a chain of formal reasoning against a chain of thought.

Where the research plan says *where possible add another symbolic backend*, the answer is
[P2.8](../p2.8_representations/README.md), later and on its own.

## Acceptance

- **The minimal goal's two sides are two records of the same run** ([Q-M2.17](../open_questions/milestone/Q-M2.17-what-is-the-milestones-minimal-goal.md)): on the Zebra puzzle's text, B1,
  the model alone, with its context and budget stated, beside the arms that use Ein.
- Seven arms as seven configs, each derived from one base config; a config whose arm's name does not match its
  fields (arrangement, level, strategy, thinking, grammar, prompts) is rejected.
- The budget rule is a number with a tolerance, measured on `dev`, written into each arm's config, and what each
  arm spent is a column of every table that shows it, with the kernel's work in columns of its own.
- Every arm's answer is a short text read by one rule; the rule agrees with a hand-read sample of 100 answers
  drawn across the arms at a measured rate, and the rate is stated with the main table.
- B2 to B5 read their answer from the record: what the final program printed through its query's `:goal-text`
  and EinAf's formatters, and the outcome beside it. No arm's answer is read from a terminal.
- Each arm has its pre-registered readings before any run on `val`.

## Tasks

### Task T2.5.2.1 — Seven arms, seven configs

An arm is a value of the package: a function from the experiment's base config to a record's `config.json`,
which changes only the fields that define the arm. Everything else (the model's SHA, the seeds, the split, the
commit) comes from the base config, so the seven cannot drift apart on a field that is not the variable. The
arm's name is **derived** and checked, not declared: `B4` is the filter, at F2, by repair, thinking off, the
grammar on, the contract's prompts.

### Task T2.5.2.2 — B0, B1 and B6

**B0** is the plain model, thinking off: the instance's text and its answer line (below). No specification, no
library, no example.

**B1** is the research plan's *explicit reasoning / self-reflection* and the frame's chain of thought. The
model has a thinking mode of its own ([`analysis/host.md`](../analysis/host.md)); the alternative is an
instructed chain of thought with the mode off. **The stage takes the stronger of the two on `dev` as B1**, fixed
before `val`, so that the side the others are compared with is the best text reasoning the model has, not a
weak one. B1 is then given the matched budget as *reconsider your reasoning and answer again* turns. Its answer
after every turn is in the transcript, so one episode holds B1 at its first answer and at every budget up to the
cap, and the table shows both the first and the last. A turn the budget cuts gives no answer: the last turn that
ended within it counts, and an episode whose first turn is cut has none, as B6's episode cut at its cap has
none.

**B6** is [S2.4.6](../p2.4_loop/s2.4.6_ein_as_a_tool.md)'s episode. What this stage fixes of it, so that it
compares: it thinks as B1 does and receives what B1 receives, with the tool declared and what the filter's
prompt says of Ein, so that B6 differs from B1 by the tool and the knowledge of it; the tool replies at F8, so
that B6 differs from B5 by who decides that Ein runs, by thinking and by what holds the syntax, each a field of
the record; and it runs under the same cap of generated tokens. The level the tool replies at is an
ablation ([P2.6](../p2.6_ablations/README.md)).

**The answer line** is this stage's to write, one a family, a prompt by hash like every other, and it is given
to every arm in the same words: that the answer is a sentence or two in the problem's words that says what was
asked (the user's ruling, [Q-M2.12](../open_questions/answer/Q-M2.12-what-prints-the-answer.md): *"a sentence, like zebra's"*), and the
two fixed texts an arm may give instead, one for a problem whose statements do not determine the answer and one
for a problem whose statements contradict each other, so that an arm without Ein can say what the kernel says.
The texts are EinAf's constants, the same strings the filter prints
([S2.2.5](../p2.2_formalizer/s2.2.5_from_text.md) words them; the frame's default 15). In the filter the same
line is given with the contract's query part ([S2.2.1](../p2.2_formalizer/s2.2.1_contract.md)), where it shapes
the `:goal-text`.

### Task T2.5.2.3 — The budget and the matching rule

**The unit is generated tokens an instance, thinking included**
([README § The defaults](../README.md#the-defaults-the-re-plan-took), 6): what the model generates is what its
reasoning costs, and it is what the envelope counts exactly. Prompt tokens, calls and wall are reported beside it.
**The kernel's work is recorded beside it and is not traded against it**: no arm is given fewer tokens for the
saturations it caused, and no table adds the two.

The rule has two numbers, both measured by running B5 on `dev`, per family:

- **The cap.** One cap of generated tokens an instance for every arm that iterates: it is the token budget of
  [S2.4.1](../p2.4_loop/s2.4.1_state_machine.md)'s termination, the same for B3, B4 and B5, and the cap of B6's
  episode. It is set so that it ends few of B5's episodes on `dev`, and the stage states the fraction it ended.
  An arm under the cap stops when its own policy stops it: the filter on a pass, the tool when the model answers.
- **The match.** B1 does not stop of itself, so it is **given** a budget and made to spend it: B5's mean of
  generated tokens an instance on `dev`, within a tenth, so that the two arms' totals are the same. The median
  is recorded beside the mean.

B0 and B2 are single calls and spend what they spend. What every arm **spent** is a column beside what it was
given, so a reader sees the match and does not have to trust it; if B5's spend on `val` leaves the tolerance of
what `dev` gave B1, the table says so. The unit and the tolerance are written once, here and in each config, and
the research plan's phrase *similar inference budget* means these numbers. Accuracy at an equal spend for B3
against B4 and B5, where their spends differ, is read off the transcripts' prefixes
([P2.6](../p2.6_ablations/README.md), G1 and G7).

**The sampler chain is one for every arm, and which one is this stage's**
([S2.2.2](../p2.2_formalizer/s2.2.2_model_runtime.md) leaves it here). The two candidates are the model's own
defaults, which sample, and greedy decoding. The default is the model's own chain: it is what the model's
thinking was tuned under, and under it three seeds are three runs whose spread is the table's error bar. Greedy
is run beside it on `dev` for every arm; if the order of the arms differs between the two, the table is taken
under both and says so. Under a greedy chain the seeds are one run, and the error bar is a resampling over
instances. In the filter the grammar is one more sampler of the same chain.

**The kernel's bounds are the same in every arm that runs Ein**: the default `--region-budget`, and the two
bounds every call is held to, the clock and the limit on a saturation ([S2.1.3](../p2.1_kernel_as_instrumentation/s2.1.3_feedback_object.md)), all fields of the record. A run
that any of them stops is the outcome *not decided*, in a column of its own, and is never read as one of the
other outcomes.

### Task T2.5.2.4 — The answer as a short text, and the error of reading it

Every arm's answer is scored as a short text, by one rule
([README § The defaults](../README.md#the-defaults-the-re-plan-took), 7). The reader has two parts.

**Which text is the answer.** For B0, B1 and B6: the content of the model's last message, the thinking apart.
For B2 to B5: what the final program prints, which is each binding row rendered through its query's
`:goal-text` (the library holds the rendered rows as values, `ein_infer::read_out::Class::rendered`; they are on
the read-out and in no key of `ein-summary/2`) with EinAf's formatters over the values that do not read as text
([Q-M2.12](../open_questions/answer/Q-M2.12-what-prints-the-answer.md)). What is scored where the kernel does
not say *one* (a class's fixed text, or no answer) is [S2.5.3](s2.5.3_metrics.md)'s answer rule; the record keeps
what the program printed and what the kernel found, and the rule reads them.

**The normaliser**: case, articles, punctuation, the instance's own alias list, and the two fixed texts'
accepted wordings. It is shared with [S2.5.3](s2.5.3_metrics.md), so B0 and B5 are compared by one rule.

A reader is a source of error that favours whichever side is stricter, and here no arm escapes it: in the filter
the sentence is one the model wrote as its `:goal-text`. So the reader's agreement with a **hand-read sample of
100** answers, drawn across the seven arms, is measured before the main table and stated with it. For the arms
that have a program there is a second check with no reader in it, the gold `:expect` claim on the program
([S2.5.3](s2.5.3_metrics.md)); where the two disagree the instance is read by hand, and the disagreements are
counted.

### Task T2.5.2.5 — Pre-registration and tests

Each arm carries, in the experiment's pre-registration, the sentence that says what a high or a low number under
it means: B0 high on a family, *the family is in the model*; B3 near B5, *the kernel is a retry trigger*; B4 near
B5, *F3 to F8 add nothing*; B1 at or above B5 at the matched budget, *a chain of thought does what the filter
does*; B6 near B1 with few calls, *the model does not use Ein when it may*; B6 at or above B5, *forcing the
program buys nothing*. The table is then read by rules written before it existed. Tests, in EinAf's gate step:
the reader on a fixture of 30 hand-written answers a form, the adversarial ones among them (two members of the
answer domain named, a refusal, an answer followed by a second thought).

### Task T2.5.2.6 — What the arms found of Ein

Five of the seven arms drive the kernel on programs no person wrote. What that shows of Ein goes to
[the evaluation of Ein](../evaluation/README.md): which refusals a model meets most and whether their messages let
it repair; what loads in silence and should not have; where the default budget stopped a question; any wrong
answer, which goes to [the wrong-answer register](../../wrong_answers/README.md) as well. The counts are
[S2.5.3](s2.5.3_metrics.md)'s; the cases are this stage's to keep as it meets them.
