## Context

The point at which the research question has a first answer, and **the first measurement of a chain of formal
reasoning against a chain of thought**, which is the user's core idea
([README § The two main ideas](../README.md#the-two-main-ideas-the-user-2026-11-25)). Seven arms, one model, one
split, three seeds, four metric layers and the two conditional probabilities under each reading of *certified*:
the **Level C gate's first row**, and the table every later table refines. The ablations of
[P2.6](../p2.6_ablations/README.md) add rows between its arms, the curves of
[P2.7](../p2.7_failure_scaling_generalization/README.md) slice it by a knob, and the `test` run of
[P2.10](../p2.10_result_artifact_demo/README.md) repeats exactly the arms this table's reading names.

What it is and is not evidence for is written **before it is run**, in the experiment's pre-registration, in the
research plan's own vocabulary. The three admissible shapes of Stage O are named in advance with the pattern in
the table that would select each: positive (*the filter or the tool beats a chain of thought and a blind retry at
a matched budget, on most families*), narrow (*one level does the work and the rest add nothing*), negative
(*precision of the accepted answers up, accuracy flat, because misformalization dominates*). The table is then
read by that page, and if it selects none of the three the reading says so and names the fourth.

It is `val`, and the table's caption names its split. `test` is read only by a run that names it and gives a
reason, and two openings are sanctioned, the Level B run and
[P2.10](../p2.10_result_artifact_demo/README.md)'s ([S2.3.4](../p2.3_benchmark/s2.3.4_splits.md)); this table is
neither, and its records say `opens_test: null`.

**The run is an experiment, by hand, in no tier**
([README § The defaults](../README.md#the-defaults-the-re-plan-took), 4): it needs the model's file and the GPU,
and no runner has either. What a gate holds of this stage is what needs no model.

## Acceptance

- The pre-registration is committed **before** the first record has a generation, and names: the seven arms by
  the digest of their configs, the split's digest, the model's file by its SHA, the sampler chain and the three
  seeds, the budget's two numbers measured on `dev`, the answer rule and the readings of *certified* the table
  will show, and the three shapes with their selecting patterns.
- The table: B0 to B6, by family, by gold class and as a macro aggregate, with H1 to H4; `P(correct | certified)`
  and `P(faithful | certified)` under each reading, with denominators; the seven outcomes of the arms that run
  Ein, the flagged in a column of its own; what each arm spent beside what it was given, with the kernel's work
  in columns of its own; the reader's measured agreement. Generated by `einaf report` under
  `einaf/experiments/reports/`, with its footer of record ids, and committed.
- `einaf report` regenerates the committed table **byte for byte** from the committed records, and a test of
  **EinAf's gate step** holds that. It is not a test of Ein's: Ein's tests read only Ein.
- The reading: one page, the pattern matched, the shape selected or none, and the provisional answer to the
  question of G1 and G2, *does B4 exceed B3 at a matched budget*, marked provisional and handed to
  [P2.6](../p2.6_ablations/README.md)'s S2.6.1, which pre-registers and confirms it.
- Nothing was tuned between the pre-registration and the table: the prompt hashes, the model's SHA and the
  repository's commit in the records equal the ones the pre-registration names.
- [The evaluation of Ein](../ein_evaluation.md) has what the run found, and it is shown with the table.

## Tasks

### Task T2.5.4.1 — The pre-registration

The experiment's `README.md`, first. The arms and the split by digest, the budget's two numbers, the sampler
chain and the seeds, the answer rule, and the shapes with their selecting patterns written as inequalities over
the table's cells. For example, *positive*:
`acc(B5) − acc(B1) > δ` **and** `acc(B5) − acc(B3) > δ` on most families, the same with B6 in B5's place read
beside it; *negative*: `P(correct | certified) − acc(B5) > δ` **and** `acc(B5) − acc(B1) ≤ δ`. The inequalities
that name *certified* are written once for each reading, since no reading is selected
([Q-M2.10](../open_questions.md#q-m210--what-does-ein-certifies-a-unique-model-mean-on-r)): a shape that holds
under one reading and not under another is reported as that. **δ and *most* are the stage's to fix**, from the
spread B5 shows on `dev`, stated here and not moved. The fourth outcome, none matched, is written as a
possibility, with the instruction that it is reported as such.

### Task T2.5.4.2 — The budget from `dev`

B5 on `dev`, three seeds, per family: the cap of generated tokens an instance and the fraction of B5's
episodes it ended, and B5's mean spend with the median beside it. The cap goes into the configs of B3, B4, B5
and B6 and the mean into B1's, as [S2.5.2](s2.5.2_baselines.md) T2.5.2.3 says, and both into the
pre-registration. On `dev` too, B1's two forms are compared and one is fixed (T2.5.2.2), and every arm is run
under greedy decoding beside the sampler chain (T2.5.2.3). `dev` is the only split a number may be read from
before the table, and these are the readings taken from it.

### Task T2.5.4.3 — The run

`einaf run`, one record an arm, one index line each, all seven naming the same split digest, model SHA and
commit, all pre-registered by the one page (each record's `README.md` links it). Three seeds an arm. The machine
is taken into each record ([S2.5.1](s2.5.1_experiment_record.md) T2.5.1.7); the run is on AC, with nothing else
holding the GPU's memory ([`analysis/host.md`](../analysis/host.md)). A run that fails part-way is `abandoned`
in the index and run again under a new id, never resumed into the same directory. After the run:
`einaf readings`, `einaf kernel-cost`, `einaf evaluate`, each on the records' own commit.

### Task T2.5.4.4 — The table and its reading

`einaf report` with the seven record ids; the table committed; the reading written as one page in the report's
directory: the pattern matched, the shape, the provisional answer to G1 and G2, the three comparisons the phase
names (B5 and B6 against B1; B5 against B6; B3, B4, B5), the families where the picture differs from the
aggregate (the pre-registration names which it expects, from what the catalogue offers each family: where it
offers no theory the model has to write the rules), and the gap of the two probabilities under each reading in
one sentence each. Where the reader's error is large enough to move a comparison, the reading says so.

### Task T2.5.4.5 — What is handed over

Written here and handed to the coordinator, not inserted by this stage: for the milestone's README and
[`plans/README.md`](../../README.md)'s table, the record ids, the shape selected, and the one sentence the table
supports, with no date as a status. It is the sentence
[P2.7](../p2.7_failure_scaling_generalization/README.md) carries to the Level C gate and
[P2.10](../p2.10_result_artifact_demo/README.md) tests on `test`. With it, for the user: the table of readings
by family and shape that [Q-M2.10](../open_questions.md#q-m210--what-does-ein-certifies-a-unique-model-mean-on-r)
is decided on.

### Task T2.5.4.6 — What the run found of Ein

The first run in which the kernel answers thousands of programs it was not written against.
[The evaluation of Ein](../ein_evaluation.md) takes: the numbers of [S2.5.3](s2.5.3_metrics.md) T2.5.3.6 for
this run; each wrong answer, also filed in [the wrong-answer register](../../wrong_answers/README.md); each
limit met, with its program; and what the kernel did well, stated as plainly as what it did not (a refusal a
model repaired from at once is a strength with a count). It is shown at the phase's close with the table
([Q-M2.15](../open_questions.md#q-m215--where-does-m2-stop-for-the-next-iteration-of-ein)).

## Notes

- If the budget's measurement on `dev` shows B5's spend varying by an order of magnitude across families, the
  matching rule is applied per family and the table says so; one number that matches nobody is not used.
- What EinAf's per-commit step re-derives is decided by what it costs: the report from the records' derived
  files always; `einaf evaluate` over every committed record again if that fits the step, and by hand at a
  level's gate if it does not.
- The table is one kernel's: every record names one commit, and `einaf report` refuses to mix two without saying
  so in the caption ([S2.5.1](s2.5.1_experiment_record.md) T2.5.1.4).
