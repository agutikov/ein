## Context

The point at which the research question has a first answer. Six
conditions, one model, one split, three seeds, four metric layers and the
two conditional probabilities — the **Level C gate's first row**, and the
table every later table is a refinement of: the ablations of
[P2.6](../p2.6_ablations/README.md) add rows between B3 and B5, the curves
of [P2.7](../p2.7_failure_scaling_generalization/README.md) slice it by a
knob, and the `test` run of [P2.10](../p2.10_result_artifact_demo/README.md)
repeats exactly the conditions this table's reading names.

What it is and is not evidence for is written **before it is run**, in the
experiment's pre-registration `README.md`, in the plan's own vocabulary:
the three admissible shapes of Stage O — positive (*B5 > B1, B3 at matched
budget on most families*), narrow (*one level does the work and the rest
add nothing*), negative (*precision up, accuracy flat, because
misformalization dominates*) — are named in advance with the pattern in
the table that would select each. The table is then read by that page, and
if it selects none of the three the reading says so and names the fourth.

It is `val`. The `test` split stays frozen and unopened; the table's caption
says which split it is on, and [S2.3.4](../p2.3_benchmark/s2.3.4_splits.md)'s
rule means the harness cannot open `test` without the flag only
[S2.10.1](../p2.10_result_artifact_demo/README.md) is allowed to pass.

## Acceptance

- The pre-registration `README.md` is committed **before** `exp-NNNN` has a
  single generation, and names: the six conditions by config digest, the
  split digest, the model SHA, the three seeds, the budget rule's measured
  number from `dev`, and the three readings with their selecting patterns.
- The table: B0–B5 × (per family, per verdict class, macro aggregate) ×
  H1–H4, plus `P(correct | certified)` and `P(faithful | certified)` with
  denominators, plus the actual-spend column beside the budget column, plus
  the extractor-error footnote — generated by `einaf report … --table main`
  under `experiments/_reports/main-val-v1/`, with its record-id footer, and
  committed.
- `einaf report` regenerates the committed table **byte-identically** from
  the records; the test that checks this runs in the gate.
- The reading: one page, the pattern matched, the shape selected (or none),
  and the provisional answer to G1 / G2's question — *does B4 exceed B3 at
  matched budget?* — marked provisional and handed to
  [S2.6.1](../p2.6_ablations/README.md), which pre-registers and confirms it.
- Nothing was tuned between the pre-registration and the table: the prompt
  hashes, the model SHA and the Ein commit in the record equal the ones the
  pre-registration names.

## Tasks

### Task T2.5.4.1 — The pre-registration

`experiments/exp-NNNN/README.md`, first. The conditions by digest, the
split by digest, the budget number, the seeds, and the three shapes with
their selecting patterns written as inequalities over the table's cells —
e.g. *positive*: `acc(B5) − acc(B3) > δ` on ≥ 5 of 8 families **and**
`P(correct | certified)` ≥ `acc(B5)` + margin; *negative*:
`P(correct | certified) − acc(B5) > δ` **and** `acc(B5) − acc(B1) ≤ δ`. The
δ is stated here and not moved. The fourth outcome — none matched — is
written as a possibility, with the instruction that it is reported as such.

### Task T2.5.4.2 — The budget from `dev`

B5 on `dev`, three seeds, per family: median output tokens per task. The
number goes into B1's and B3's configs as [S2.5.2](s2.5.2_baselines.md)
T2.5.2.3 says, and into the pre-registration. `dev` is the only split a
number may be read from before the table; this is the one reading.

### Task T2.5.4.3 — The run

`einaf run` six times, or once with six conditions — one record per
condition, one index line each, all six naming the same split digest, model
SHA and Ein commit, all six pre-registered by the one page (the README of
each record links the shared page rather than copying it). Three seeds per
condition; the spread is the table's error bar. Machine state captured per
record by [`bench_env.sh`](../../../utils/bench_env.sh). A run that fails
mid-way is `abandoned` in the index and re-run under a new id — never
resumed into the same directory.

### Task T2.5.4.4 — The table and its reading

`einaf report` with the six record ids; the table committed; the reading
written as one page in the report directory — the pattern matched, the
shape, the provisional G1 / G2 answer, the families where the picture
differs from the aggregate (expected: the two families with no stdlib
theory, object tracking and temporal, where B2 has nothing to select and
the loop has to synthesise), and the two probabilities' gap in one
sentence. Where the extractor error rate of B0 / B1 is large enough to move
a comparison, the reading says so.

### Task T2.5.4.5 — The status paragraph

A paragraph for the milestone README's **Status** line and for
[`plans/README.md`](../../README.md)'s table: the date, the record ids, the
shape selected, the one sentence the table supports — written here, handed
over, not inserted by this stage. It is also the sentence
[P2.7](../p2.7_failure_scaling_generalization/README.md) will carry to the
Level C gate and [P2.10](../p2.10_result_artifact_demo/README.md) will test
on `test`.

## Notes

- Three seeds × six conditions × the `val` split at
  [S2.3.4](../p2.3_benchmark/s2.3.4_splits.md)'s sizes is the first run in
  the milestone whose wall is days rather than minutes; the record's
  `machine.txt` and the envelopes' timings are what let the cost be stated
  rather than remembered.
- If the budget measurement on `dev` shows B5's spend varying by an order
  of magnitude across families, the matching rule is applied per family
  and the table says so — not averaged into one number that matches nobody.
