## Context

A grammar holds every token to a text that can still become a program; it does not end one. Greedy decoding that
has entered a cycle stays in it, and the call runs to its budget. The stage begins by checking what this file says
against the tree: what a record holds of a generation (its tokens, or its text alone, which the slim records of
S2.2.5 keep), what stop reasons the envelope has, and what the binding's sampler chain offers.

## Acceptance

- `einaf::model` ends a generation whose tail repeats one cycle a stated number of times, with a stop reason of
  its own that names where the cycle began and its period. It is asked for by a field of the request; a request
  that names none generates as before.
- **Held with no model, on every recorded generation of P2.2**: the detector stops each generation that ran to
  its budget in a loop, and no generation that ended by itself; a test of EinAf's gate step holds both over the
  fixtures the gate has, and a script beside the stage's report over the records a host holds.
- **Held by an invariance on the one shot's eight calls**: read as S2.2.5 reads a cut output, the forms kept under
  the stop are the forms kept under the budget's cut.
- The bound on comment lines is a parameter of the step grammars, off by default; the repetition sampler, where
  the binding has one, is a value of a request's `sampler`.
- With the model, a plan committed first: the calls of S2.2.4 and S2.2.5 that ran to the budget, run again under
  the stop alone, under the stop and the bound, and under a chain with a repetition sampler ahead of greedy. Per
  call: what it wrote, where it stopped and why, its tokens and time, and the step's program judged as its stage
  judged it.

## Tasks

### Task T2.2c.1.1 — What a loop is, by the records

Every recorded generation that ran to its budget, read: where its cycle began, its period, what it repeated (a
comment line, a premise inside a form, a whole fact), and what the grammar refused before it (`chosen_again`).
From them the rule: how many repetitions of how long a cycle end a generation, chosen so that the rule fires on
each of these and on none of the generations that ended by themselves. The numbers are the stage's, recorded with
the generations that set them.

### Task T2.2c.1.2 — The stop

The detector over the generated tokens, in the loop over tokens EinAf owns; the stop reason and its two numbers
in the envelope; the field of the request, and its default, off. What a caller gets back is the generation up to
the end of the cycle's first occurrence, or to the stop; which of the two is decided by the invariance of
T2.2c.1.3.

### Task T2.2c.1.3 — The readers

`einaf::formalize` reads a stop on a loop as it reads a cut by the budget (the forms written before it kept, the
form it stopped in dropped), and says which of the two stopped the step. Thinking on, a loop inside the thinking
never reaches `</think>`: whether EinAf then closes the thinking itself is [P2.2b](../p2.2b_guided_thinking/README.md)'s
*budgeted* way, and this stage gives it the stop to build on.

### Task T2.2c.1.4 — The bound on comment lines, and the repetition sampler

**The bound**: so many comment lines in a row, a parameter of each step's grammar. With no model: at which token
it would have refused each recorded loop in comments, and that every step by hand and every example is still
accepted. **The sampler**: what the binding exposes of llama.cpp's samplers against repetition is looked up in the
crate and recorded; a chain with one ahead of greedy stays one run a request.

### Task T2.2c.1.5 — The calls that looped, run again

The plan: the fifteen calls, the three conditions, written before the first generation. The report is the stage's
record: a row a call and a condition, and whether a stop, a bound or a sampler turned a loop into a program, into
another loop, or into nothing.
