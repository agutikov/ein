**Phase:** [P2.2c](README.md) (A generation that ends, and the model alone)
**Status:** planned 2026-11-27; not started.
**Depends on:** [S2.2c.1](s2.2c.1_a_generation_that_ends.md): the stop on a loop;

the other side of the minimal goal under the model's own sampler, and whether the model reasons or recalls.

## Context

One record says what the model does alone: the Zebra text, thinking on, greedy, the right grid in its thinking by
about its 5 900th token, then a loop to the budget and no answer
([L-M2.29](../findings/model/L-M2.29-the-model-alone-solves-the-zebra-puzzle-in-its-thinking-and-names-it.md)). It
was run on that one text and under one decoding. This stage runs the few runs that say on which texts the model
fails alone, which is where help can be measured at all.

## Acceptance

With the model, every run a record, **the plan of runs committed before its first generation**:

- **The texts**: `nl2smt/puzzles/zebra.txt`, `zebra-minus-15.txt` and `zebra-contradiction.txt`; the Zebra puzzle
  with every noun replaced and the sentences reordered; and one puzzle of the same size and the same kinds of
  clue that is not the Zebra puzzle. For each of the two new texts a program by hand in the target shape, which
  the kernel answers *one* with the gold sentence.
- **The decodings**, thinking on: the model's own chain, seeded, a stated count of seeds; and greedy with the stop
  on a loop. Thinking off, greedy, once a text, for what the model answers with no reasoning.
- **Per run**: the stop and its reason; the thinking's tokens and the answer's; the answer as given, read by the
  reader and by hand; whether the grid in the thinking is right where no answer is printed; whether the thinking
  names the puzzle or brings kinds no sentence states.
- **The table**: a text by a decoding, solved *k of n*; on the two edits, whether the model says *not determined*
  and *contradictory* or gives the original's answer.
- **The report says where the minimal goal's premise stands**, and
  [Q-M2.39](../open_questions/milestone/Q-M2.39-what-is-the-minimal-goal-s-instance-if-the-model-alone-solves-the-zebra-text.md)
  carries the measurement.

## Tasks

### Task T2.2c.2.1 — The two new texts

**The renamed puzzle**: a map of every noun of the Zebra text to a word that is no nationality, colour, drink,
pet or cigarette of the original, the sentences in another order, the questions kept; the hand's program with the
same map, answered by the kernel. **The unseen puzzle**: five places, five kinds, clues of the same sorts (a
value in a place, two values together, next to, immediately right of), written so that it has one answer, which
the kernel finds on a program by hand and `ein solve --maximal` confirms is the only one. Both texts and both
programs are files of the stage, listed for [S2.3.4](../p2.3_benchmark/s2.3.4_splits.md) as seen by development.

### Task T2.2c.2.2 — The plan, and the runs

The texts, the decodings, the seeds, the budget of generated tokens and the context, written by a script beside
the record before the first generation, as S2.2.2's plan was. The prompt holds the text and the answer line and
nothing of Ein.

### Task T2.2c.2.3 — The report

The stage's folder, with a README that is its report: the table; each text, with the thinking quoted where it
names the puzzle, reads *next to*, backtracks or loses a grid it had; and what it says of the minimal goal.

### Task T2.2c.2.4 — What the stage found

Of the model, to [the phase's findings](findings/README.md). For the user: Q-M2.39 with the texts the model fails
alone on, and [Q-M2.27](../open_questions/model/Q-M2.27-do-the-phase-s-thinking-runs-stay-greedy-when-greedy-thinking-loops.md)
with what the model's own chain did to the loops. For [S2.5.2](../p2.5_harness/s2.5.2_baselines.md): what B1's
default would have to say of thinking, the chain and the budget, which it decides on `dev`.
