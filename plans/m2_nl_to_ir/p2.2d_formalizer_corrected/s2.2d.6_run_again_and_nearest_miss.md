## Context

Three things are left when the contract, the cut, the entries, the check and the grammars are corrected: what
only a prompt can say; the same 29 runs again, to see what the corrections did to the programs; and the one thing
P2.2's own risk list asked for and the phase did not measure, *the nearest miss step by step*
([L-M2.24](../findings/model/L-M2.24-the-model-s-zebra-program-has-seven-defects-and-three-repairs-leave-r-empty.md)).

## Acceptance

- **The prompts' next version**, each part versioned and hashed as B4 asks:
  - *a description is not a new thing*, one paragraph of the step-1 template with the wrong form beside the right
    one: *the red house* is one of the five houses, described by its colour: `Red`, a `Color`, never `RedHouse`,
    a sixth house. It states the target shape's abbreviation as the rule it is
    ([Q-M2.38](../open_questions/formalizer/Q-M2.38-is-a-description-abbreviated-to-its-word-or-written-as-a-term.md)'s
    default);
  - **an example with a change over time**: none of the three examples has one, and seven of the thirteen word
    texts hold a giving; the steps by hand write the moments (`Start`, `After`). An example of another text that
    does the same, under S2.2.4's family rule.
- **The categories of a program the kernel refuses**, computed from the parse
  ([L-M2.26](../findings/einaf/L-M2.26-a-program-the-kernel-refuses-carries-no-categories.md)): every form of
  every recorded program in exactly one row, held on P2.2's seven refused programs.
- **The plan's 29 run again**, unchanged but for the formalizer and the stop on a loop, beside P2.2's 29, with the
  reader's two counts: **a development number, labelled so in every table**.
- **The nearest miss, by hand and with no model**: the model's Zebra program of P2.2 mended one defect at a time,
  the seven of L-M2.24's table in the order a reader of the kernel's word would meet them; after each, the
  outcome, the core with its sentences, H's writer and size, and the check's findings, old and new. It ends at the
  least edit that prints the two names.
- **The phase's report**, for the user's review: what each stage changed, the commands that show it with their
  outputs, the two runs side by side, the ladder, and every default taken.
