# S2.2f.2 — The analysis written for the formalizer

**Phase:** [P2.2f](README.md) (The text analysed by trained networks before it is formalized)
**Status:** planned 2026-11-27; not started.
**Needs:** [S2.2f.1](s2.2f.1_the_check.md)'s verdict: only what passed the check is written here.
**Reads:** the steps' prompts and their slots ([`docs/einaf/07-steps.md`](../../../docs/einaf/07-steps.md));
[M1k's P1k.20](../../m1k_notations_as_facts/p1k.20_s_expressions_as_facts/README.md), S-expressions as Ein facts.

## Context

What passed the check has to reach the model. Two ways, both measured by S2.2f.3: **as text in the prompt**, the
graph as the parser printed it or as plain lines, a node a line, which the model reads as context and cannot copy
into its program; and **as Ein facts**, a vocabulary of the analysis's own (a node, its concept, a role between two
nodes, a name, a polarity), each fact with the sentence it came from as its `:source`, which the kernel loads, `ein
render kb` draws, and step 1 could be given as a program to read.

## Acceptance

- The two forms, written by a committed script from the parser's output; the facts load under `ein solve
  --no-search` with no refusal, and `ein render kb` draws them. What of AMR's spelling Ein's names do not take
  (a role `:ARG0`, a sense `drink-01`, a quoted name) and how it is written instead, stated once.
- The alignment: each node with its sentence, and with its words where an aligner gives them; how many nodes have
  none, per text.
- EinAf reads an analysis from a file (a slot of the steps' prompts, a flag of `einaf steps run` and `einaf
  from-text`), and its tests replay the recorded analyses of the six texts, committed under
  `einaf/tests/fixtures/`, with no Python and no GPU.
- What the facts cost the kernel (facts, a load's time) on the six texts; anything of Ein to the evaluation.
- The gate is green on the stage's last commit.

## Tasks

### Task T2.2f.2.1 — The text form

The graph as the parser prints it, and a list of lines; the default is the one the prompt's budget holds for the
Zebra text. A prompt template with the analysis slot, by hash like every other.

### Task T2.2f.2.2 — The facts form

The vocabulary, its declarations with their `:why`, the converter, and the six texts converted and loaded. Not a
module of Ein's library: the analysis's vocabulary is EinAf's, and nothing of Ein names it.

### Task T2.2f.2.3 — EinAf reads it

The slot, the flag, the record's field (the tool, its version, the analysis's hash), the tests.

### Task T2.2f.2.4 — What the stage found

To the phase's ledgers; runnable commands with their output for the phase report.
