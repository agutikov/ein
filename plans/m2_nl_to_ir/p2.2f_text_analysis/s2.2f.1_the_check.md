## Context

The model's step 1 goes wrong on the reading of the sentence: a multi-word name split, a passive made a second
active relation, a placement no sentence states, a number out of its fact, a polarity or a kind wrong in silence.
Trained parsers read sentences for a living. Before anything is built, **read what they write on the same six
texts, by hand, against the hand's step 1**, and see whether they carry what the model dropped. The check needs no
model and no formalizer, and it decides whether the phase goes on.

## Acceptance

- **An environment on this host**, made by a committed script (`setup.sh` in this folder, or the phase's tools
  folder), under the gitignored `build/nlp/`, every cache there and nothing under `~`
  ([the phase's § The environment](README.md#the-environment)). What did not install, and why.
- **Each tool's peak GPU memory**, alone on the card (`nvidia-smi` read before and during), and whether it fits 8 GB;
  its version and its model's name and hash.
- **AMR from at least two parsers** on every sentence of the six texts, and beside them the analyses the survey
  finds that install and fit: a Universal Dependencies parse, coreference across sentences, word senses. Each
  sentence's outputs kept under the stage's `runs/` (not committed), and a rendering of each AMR graph a reader can
  look at.
- **The table** (committed, `check/README.md` or the stage's record): for each text, each sentence, each failure
  class below, each tool: *right*, *wrong* or *not carried*, against the hand's step 1, with the model's step 1 in a
  column beside it. Read by hand; where a cell is a judgement, the note says why.
- **A verdict per tool**: it goes on to S2.2f.2 or it does not, and why. The default: it goes on if it fits 8 GB and
  is right on more cells of the table than the model's step 1 was.

### The failure classes

Read from what P2.2 found; a class with no case in a text is marked so.

| class | what is right | where P2.2 saw it go wrong |
|---|---|---|
| a multi-word name | one name, one concept: *Old Gold*, *Lucky Strike* | the Zebra puzzle, split into two arguments |
| active and passive | one relation, the roles the same both ways: *drinks* and *is drunk by* | the Zebra puzzle, the passive written as a second active relation |
| negation | the polarity of the sentence | L-M2.18 |
| a thing's kind | the noun's concept: a person, a house, a pet, a box | L-M2.18 |
| a number | the value inside its fact, as the sentence states it | the word problems: out of its fact, or a name |
| what no sentence states | nothing: a placement or a fact the text does not say is absent | the seating |
| coreference | *he*, *the man who …*, *the one* resolved to the thing they name | mostly right in P2.2; checked, not assumed |
| an order or a place | *older than*, *next to*, *to the left of*, *in the middle*: a relation with its arguments in order | the Zebra puzzle's *next to*, the seating, `youngest` |
| the question | what is asked, and of what | L-M2.21's query asking one person both questions |

## Tasks

### Task T2.2f.1.1 — The survey and the environment

What exists and installs here, by layer: AMR (at least two parsers; amrlib's models, IBM's transition-AMR parser,
SPRING, and whatever else fits 8 GB, a parser built on a language model included), a Universal Dependencies parser
(spaCy's transformer model, Stanza, UDPipe), coreference, word senses. Each tried in the one environment; what
fails, with its error. Link grammar, NLTK's grammars and any hand-written grammar of English are not candidates
([Q11](../open_questions/formalizer/Q11-link-grammar-value.md)).

### Task T2.2f.1.2 — The runs

Every sentence of the six texts through every tool that installed, by a committed script: a text in, each tool's
output per sentence out, under `runs/`, with the tool's version and the GPU memory it took. An AMR graph drawn
(PENMAN as written, and a picture) so the table can be read against it.

### Task T2.2f.1.3 — The table

By hand, sentence by sentence, class by class, as above; the hand's step 1 the reference, the model's step 1 the
column it is compared with. A short note per text on what the tools got that the model did not, and the reverse.

### Task T2.2f.1.4 — What the stage found

The verdict per tool; what the next stage takes; to [the phase's findings](findings/README.md) what it found of the
tools and the host; to [the evaluation](evaluation/README.md) anything of Ein (a parse that reads as facts the
kernel cannot load, for instance). Runnable commands with their output for the phase report. **If no tool passes,
the phase stops here and reports.**
