open. The questions of [the table](README.md#the-questions) for those steps ask what a selection is for before the
model makes one: a group given whole, a row, a one-to-one assignment, a change over time, a dependency. The stage
runs them, and the program written in one call with every question, and ends with what the phase found as the
user's question.
