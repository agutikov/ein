# S2.2b.2 — Step 1 with guided thinking

**Phase:** [P2.2b](README.md) (Guided thinking)
**Status:** planned 2026-11-26; not started.
**Depends on:** [S2.2b.1](s2.2b.1_questions_by_hand.md): the questions, the analyses by hand, the scaffold, the
thinking's grammar; [S2.2.4](../p2.2_formalizer/s2.2.4_passes.md): step 1's prompt, its grammar in its fence, the
contract's check, the steps' command, and its runs with thinking off, which are this stage's baseline;
[S2.2.2](../p2.2_formalizer/s2.2.2_model_runtime.md): the runtime, the envelope, the detection of a loop.
**Carries:** the user's *"not solving the entire problem but decomposing it into parts suitable for conversion
into Ein"*, measured where the model did worst.

## Context

Step 1 is the step a model got least right with thinking off (the phase's
[starting facts](README.md#what-p22-found-that-this-phase-starts-from)), and the step whose decisions the user's
list names. It needs no catalogue, so its prompt is short, and a thinking left alone on it looped in S2.2.3. It is
the cheapest place to see whether guided thinking ends, whether it decomposes rather than solves, and whether what
it decomposes into is a better step 1.

## Acceptance

With the model, one shot a condition, every run a record, **the plan of runs committed before its first
generation**:

- **The items**: the Zebra text, the sixteen word problems (an item's text the comment at the head of its file),
  and S2.2.1's six texts.
- **The conditions**, each with step 1's prompt of S2.2.4 and its grammar after the thinking:
  - **off**: thinking off, S2.2.4's runs, taken again only if their inputs changed;
  - **alone**: thinking on, nothing guiding it;
  - **instructed**, **scaffolded**, **held to the grammar**: the [three ways](README.md#the-four-ways-to-guide-the-thinking);
  - **budgeted**: a stated budget of thinking tokens, with *alone* and with the strongest of the three that ends
    least often.
- **Per run**:
  - the stop, and where a loop began and its period;
  - the thinking's tokens and the answer's, and the wall time;
  - **whether the thinking solved**: a value of the answer, known for each item, stated in the thinking; read off
    every run, and by hand on a stated sample;
  - the analysis against the hand's, where there is one (things, coreferences, kinds, relations with their
    places, quantities, the question);
  - the program: parse, load, the contract's check (a `:source` no sentence, a sentence with no fact, the answer
    query), root's judgement, and the hand's step 1 facts matched.
- **The comparison** with *off*, per item and summed, at the whole cost in generated tokens.

## Tasks

### Task T2.2b.2.1 — The plan

The items, the conditions, the budgets, the bounds of the thinking's grammar, the sample read by hand and its
seed, written into the record before the first generation, by a script beside the record, as S2.2.2's and S2.2.3's
plans were. The examples are S2.2.4's rule: never of the item's own family; an example of guided thinking is an
analysis by hand of another family's text.

### Task T2.2b.2.2 — The runs

Through the steps' command of S2.2.4 with a thinking mode added, greedy, the model `qwen3.5:4b`, the grammar's
token checked by the chain's choice first
([Q-M2.28](../open_questions/model/Q-M2.28-is-a-token-held-to-its-grammar-by-the-chain-s-choice-first.md)'s
default). Replay of every record with no model in EinAf's gate step, through the parse, the check and the
comparison.

### Task T2.2b.2.3 — The report

The records' folder, `plans/m2_nl_to_ir/p2.2b_guided_thinking/step1/`, with a README that is the stage's report:
a table a condition, then each item, then every run that solved, looped or wrote a better or a worse step 1 than
*off*, with its thinking quoted where it says why.

### Task T2.2b.2.4 — What the stage found

Of Ein, to [the evaluation](../evaluation/README.md): what root's judgement said of a guided step 1 and of an
unguided one. Of the model and EinAf, to the phase's findings: whether a thinking with a shape still loops under
greedy decoding, which bears on
[Q-M2.27](../open_questions/model/Q-M2.27-do-the-phase-s-thinking-runs-stay-greedy-when-greedy-thinking-loops.md).
