# S2.2b.1 — The questions, the analysis by hand, and the thinking's grammar

**Phase:** [P2.2b](README.md) (Guided thinking)
**Status:** planned 2026-11-26; not started.
**Depends on:** [S2.2.1](../p2.2_formalizer/s2.2.1_contract.md): the six texts and their steps by hand,
`einaf/contract/`; [S2.2.3](../p2.2_formalizer/s2.2.3_gbnf.md): the step grammars, the fenced frame, the grammar
begun at `</think>`, the narrowed root, EinAf's acceptor and sampler, `einaf grammar llama`.
**Carries:** [the questions](README.md#the-questions), the user's list made into what a thinking answers.

## Context

Before the model is asked anything, the stage does by hand what the model will be asked to do, as S2.2.1 did for
the steps: an analysis of each text, written as the questions ask it, and held to the program the steps by hand
wrote from the same text. If an analysis by hand cannot be written to the questions, or does not determine the
hand's step 1, the questions are wrong, and that is found here at no model's cost.

## Acceptance

- The questions, by step, are versioned files under `einaf/prompts/`, each heading with the instruction for its
  section and the part of the program it decides ([the table](README.md#the-questions)).
- An analysis by hand for each of S2.2.1's six texts (`youngest`, `zebra`, `seating`, `apples`, `together`,
  `more`), under `einaf/contract/<text>/`, beside the steps.
- **Each analysis determines its step 1**: every name, membership, declaration and fact of the hand's step 1 is
  one the analysis lists, and every thing, kind and relation the analysis lists is one step 1 writes. A test holds
  both directions; what one holds and the other does not is a finding of the questions.
- The thinking's grammar, one a step, and joined to the step's grammar: the analysis, `</think>`, the fenced
  program. It accepts each analysis by hand joined to its step by hand; texts sampled from it are analyses of the
  questions' shape; llama.cpp's sampler reads it as EinAf's acceptor does (S2.2.3's test 3, by hand, with a record).
- The scaffold, a versioned file: the text the assistant's turn is begun with.

## Tasks

### Task T2.2b.1.1 — The questions

Each of [the table's](README.md#the-questions) rows a heading and one sentence, in the step's order; each step's
file holds its own rows. The opening instruction says what the thinking is for and what it is not: decide what the
text is made of; do not decide who or what, which is the kernel's. A section may be answered *none*.

### Task T2.2b.1.2 — The analysis by hand

For each text, the answers as the questions ask them, one item a line, **a thing written as the name the program
spells it**, its words in the text quoted beside it (the phase's design question 2). Then the comparison with the
hand's step 1, mechanical where the names make it so: things to names, kinds to memberships, relations with their
places to declarations, sentences to facts by `:source`. For the Zebra puzzle the analysis is of `zebra3.1.ein`'s
vocabulary, the target shape; its structure sections name what step 2 selects (the shared house, the row, the
one-to-one assignments).

### Task T2.2b.1.3 — The thinking's grammar

Written by `einaf::grammar` beside the step grammars: the headings in order, each followed by up to a stated
number of lines, each line of up to a stated length; a line's text is free but for the narrowed root's four
characters (`<`, `>`, `|`, the backtick), so no marker of the chat template is written in the thinking. Then
`</think>`, then the step's grammar in its fence. The bounds are the stage's to choose, recorded with what they
cost an analysis by hand (none may be cut). The grammar begins at the first token after the scaffold, so the
template's own `<think>` is the prompt's, not the grammar's.

### Task T2.2b.1.4 — Tests

In EinAf's gate step, no model: the questions' files and the scaffold by their hashes; each analysis by hand
accepted, joined to its step by hand; the comparison of T2.2b.1.2 in both directions; texts sampled from each
joined grammar parse, their analysis of the questions' shape and their program accepted by the narrowed root.

### Task T2.2b.1.5 — What the stage found

Of the questions: a decision the hand's step 1 makes that no question asks, and a question no step uses. Of Ein,
to [the evaluation](../evaluation/README.md): a part of the analysis no form of Ein holds.
