## Context

The decision: *a relation may be declared more than once, at one count or at several, and no declaration is
refused as a duplicate of another*. A program with two declarations of one relation loads; the same program with
one `(import …)` line is refused, `kb load error: conflicting definitions of 'likes': same name, different body`.
The refusal is the imports pass's de-duplication of declarations, keyed on the kind and the name, which runs only
where a program imports. The test that holds the decision has no import in its programs. Met twice by the
application: at two counts (S2.2.0a's probe) and at one count with two label lists (the model's `unused`).

## Acceptance

- The probes' two programs
  ([`probes/language/`](../p2.2_formalizer/zebra_formulations/probes/README.md): `declared-at-two-counts.ein` and
  the same with an import) answer alike; so does a relation declared twice at one count with two label lists.
- `declared_counts.rs` holds each of its cases with an import as without.
- What the pass is for still holds: a module reached by two paths comes once, and a rule or a macro declared
  twice with two bodies is refused as the page says.
- The diagnostics page's row says what is refused, and of whom; every reader of a declaration reads each
  (`ein_core::Program::declarations`), with an import as without: the blind enumerator's counts and
  `std.contradiction`'s activation, held by a test.
- The gate is green; `import_invariance.rs` reads the new cases under its three import forms.
