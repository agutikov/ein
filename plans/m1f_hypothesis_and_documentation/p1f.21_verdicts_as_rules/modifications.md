## H. Open facts

Q-M1f.390's option (d), and the user's form that takes it further
([`open_facts.md` § 4](open_facts.md#4-open-f-the-hypothesis-itself) to
[§ 6](open_facts.md#6-what-rules-can-do-with-open-facts)). **Not ruled, and not run** but as mocks and stand-ins
([item 32 § 3](review/32-Q-M1f.390/README.md#3-the-options)).

30. **(d) alone: `(open R j)` is a fact of the state that owes it**, written by the pass after the fixpoint into a
    part of the node no fork inherits, and matched by no rule. The build
    ([§ 4.6](design.md#46-what-it-costs-and-what-it-buys)): that part of a node; the pass writing facts with a
    derivation record; the grammar and the loader taking the fact in a claim and refusing it elsewhere; the
    renderers and the dumps; a test that no saturation plan can match it. What it asks of the copy-on-write layers
    and of the `.einb` container was not read. It is the one option under which a state's facts stop growing with
    its commitment. **State: option**, which the survey recommends against for that.
31. **`(open F J)`: an obligation emits the hypothesis itself, with the debt it would pay.** F is a candidate and J
    the instance of item 15, a nested fact: `(open (seats Ann C1) (total-owed seats is-a Guest Chair Ann))`. $H$ is
    then held, under `open`: one fact a candidate and debt, 8 at the seats program's root, 112 at
    `zebra2-obligations`'. A debt is a disjunction of open facts, and the debts a state owes are the J of its open
    facts it has not paid. `ein show hyps`, the read-out and `-p` print them. **State: the user's form**, beside
    (d).
32. **The move.** `(open F J)` is in a state where F is a candidate, the state holds neither F nor `(not F)`, and
    it has not paid J ([§ 5](open_facts.md#5-the-move-open-held-not)). A child's open facts are its parent's less
    what the child decides, taken in one step after the child's fixpoint: into held, by the commitment or a rule;
    into `not`, by a rule only; or out, where another candidate paid J. The move is the kernel's. No rule moves an
    open fact without deciding it: one that dropped a candidate unproven would change $R$. **State: the design**
    for 31.
33. **One form for the ladder's three rungs.**
    - `hrule` becomes a rule that concludes `(open F)`, still activated by `(solve :hrules …)`; the declarator
      goes (item 5 has the count).
    - A hypothesis of an hrule or of the blind rung pays no debt: `(open F)` alone, or J the hrule's instance.
    - `:hypothesis-relations` and `:no-hypothesis` filter what is proposed, and become rules if proposing does.
    - `__closed__` is derived, a relation no rule proposes a fact of being closed, or becomes the kernel's
      `ein.closed`.

    **State: direction** ([§ 8.2](open_facts.md#82-each-name-under-this-phases-direction)).
34. **A stratum for the rules that read open facts**, after the held fixpoint. Such a rule concludes another open
    fact or a kernel verb, **never a held fact**: a held fact concluded from an open one would stay held in the
    states above, where the open fact is gone. `open` becomes a word a `:match` and a conclusion may write.
    *Re-run*: the fact `(open (s a b))` is refused at the parse today, *open is reserved*. **State: the design**
    for 31.
35. **The rules over open facts** ([§ 6](open_facts.md#6-what-rules-can-do-with-open-facts)), which the standard
    library would state and the routes read:

    | rule | from | concludes | what does it today |
    |---|---|---|---|
    | propose | held facts | `(open F)` | an hrule; the obligations rung |
    | hopeless | a debt unpaid, none of whose candidates is open | a verdict like `(ein.kill)` | **nothing** |
    | forced | a debt with one open candidate | the next split, with one child | the tree route |
    | order | the open debts and candidates | which to split on | the tree route, in rule order |
    | ask | a question | a further question; an answer where asked | the calculator's requests |
    | report | a state of $R$ | which candidates stay open | the read-out, in part |

    *Hopeless* is a new verdict, and *hopeless* and *forced* read a count, so they are boundary rules of their
    stratum; *order* changes no $R$, only the cost. [V-6](findings.md#v-6--the-read-out-reads-is-a-by-its-name)'s
    option (b), the warning that a type was bound as an object, would be one more *report*, in a module a program
    imports. **State: to be shown** by S1f.21.4, each not to change $R$ or to be a verdict the oracle holds.

## I. The search as rules

[`open_facts.md` § 7](open_facts.md#7-the-search-as-rules): every decision of a search can be a rule, and the split
cannot, since a rule adds to the one state it runs in and a split needs states that disagree.

36. **(A) The split stays the kernel's one primitive.** A rule concludes what to split on (item 35, *order*); the
    kernel forks, saturates each child, moves its open facts and reads its verdicts. The routes keep the
    copy-on-write fork, the child resuming its parent's saturation, and the licences. **State: leaning**, for the
    engine.
37. **(B) Each state is a context**, `(in W F)` with W a fact naming the commitment. Every rule of the program is
    written again under `(in ?w …)`, which a macro can do, a rule makes the child worlds from the open facts, and
    the whole lattice is one saturation. It costs what the kernel saves: every world derives its facts again, a
    world's absences need strata per world, and it is the whole cube, $2^{|H|}$. **State: not the leaning** as an
    engine; as a statement on the pages it is item 13.
