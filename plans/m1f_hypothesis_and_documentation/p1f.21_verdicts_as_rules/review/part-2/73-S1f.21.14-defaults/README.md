S1f.21.14 is done: the pages of the second part. Its four items are on their pages, every page of `docs/ein/` and
the READMEs was read against the binary and what was stale rewritten, and the part's decisions are written. No
engine, library or corpus file changed. These are the ten defaults it took.

## What you should know first

- **A fact held neither way is *unknown*, on every page** (default 2). *Open* was the word for it; it now names
  the facts of `open` alone, and the status takes the word the library's `unknown` tests it by.
- **Twelve decision files are renamed** (default 1): each had a name that said what is no longer true. The ids are
  kept and every reference moved, the ledger's link targets among them.
- **Two decisions are written, Q-M1f.390 and Q-M1f.549, and 27 rewritten in place** where the part made them
  untrue. Q-M1f.549's file carries your main call and no decline (default 7): the two uses S1f.21.13 declined are
  a default and no decision.
- **Where the binary was short of a ruling**, the page says what the ruling says and a stage builds it:
  S1f.21.22 has the kb and constraints renders, a written `(open …)` against the lists, and the printed claim
  over `open`.
