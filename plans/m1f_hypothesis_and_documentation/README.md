## Why they are one milestone

Because the alternative was two milestones with one phase each, and because
what they have in common is the thing M1e could not give them: **neither
processes a finding.** M1e's spine is *a finding is a claim until something
holds it*; both of these start from a user's instruction and an unmeasured
idea, which is a different kind of work with a different acceptance.

**Since 2026-11-08 that is no longer true of P1f.5.** Thirteen of its stages
are M1e2's no-owner items, each a ruling of the user's on an inventory item,
and four of them fix wrong answers that S1f.5.0 filed as register rows first, W29 to W32, twenty `open`
rows on 2026-11-08, with three more from S1f.5.5's census, W33 to W35 (the register then at 103 fixed,
33 open and 1 not-wrong, `grep '^status' corpus/wrong_answers.toml | sort | uniq -c`). They
keep M1e2's discipline: a wrong answer is a row in
[`plans/wrong_answers/register.toml`](../wrong_answers/register.toml) before it is
fixed, and every move is listed before it is made.

**The order between the phases.** P1f.5 runs first. It changes the language
and moves answers, and P1f.10's acceptance, *not one model moved*, is taken
against the answers P1f.5 leaves. It also edits the kernel pages P1f.20 moves,
and P1f.20's rule is *fix first, then move*. P1f.6 writes its theory in the language P1f.5 leaves: its design
stage runs once S1f.5.5's swap has made digits names, and its theory stages after P1f.5 closes. P1f.7, added on
2026-11-10, takes the wrong answers P1f.5's re-opened stages find outside their scope, so that P1f.5 can close; it
runs after that close, and the coordinator stops there (the user: *"stop in the end of the phase"*), so where P1f.7
falls against P1f.6 is the user's call then. **P1f.5 closed on 2026-11-11**
(its close), with P1f.7 at nine stages. On 2026-11-11 the user ruled
*"p1f.7 runs before p1f.6, it's questions take a default"*: P1f.7 runs next, then P1f.6. The same day, on the
analysis of its nine findings, the user put S1f.7.8
first, moved S1f.7.9's fix to [F24](../followups/f24_written_provenance.md) and added S1f.7.10 to S1f.7.12
([Q-M1f.112](open_questions.md#q-m1f112--p1f7-after-its-analysis-the-order-s1f79-and-three-findings-with-no-stage)). **P1f.7 closed on 2026-11-11**

## Risks

- **P1f.20 is 25 days, and it closes no finding.** It was one 23-day stage,
  S1f.5.20, the largest in either milestone, until the user took its
  seven-stage split on 2026-11-08. It was blocked on three M1e stages until
  they ran on 2026-08-30/31, and nothing blocks it now.
- **P1f.10 is a CSP in disguise**, and the risk is not that the idea is wrong
  but that a stage writes a solver instead of a *structure* and the engine
  acquires a second search. The phase's own risk list is where this is argued;
  it is repeated here because it is the milestone-level failure mode.
- **The two phases can drift on the ordering question.** S1f.5.15 and S1f.10.5
  both derive an order that is currently declared by hand, in different parts
  of the engine, and nothing forces them to agree on what *derived* means.
