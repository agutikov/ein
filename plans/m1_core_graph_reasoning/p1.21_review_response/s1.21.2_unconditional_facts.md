## The claim

Three sources currently disagree:

- [`commitment.py`](../../../ein.py/src/ein/inference/commitment.py) (module
  docstring, `CommitmentSetResult.unconditional_facts:64`,
  `_is_unconditional:181`) still asserts *"derivation doesn't touch
  commitment ⇒ provably true at root"* and **computes** the extraction on
  every alive commitment.
- [`docs/kernel/inference/README.md:520-599`](../../../docs/kernel/inference/README.md)
  ("Unconditional facts — `_is_unconditional` soundness") and
  [`lattice_dump.md:101,141,169`](../../../docs/kernel/inference/lattice_dump.md)
  (`unconditional_facts.jsonl` "← facts merged back to root") still describe
  the root-merge as the live architecture.
- The live solver
  ([`monotonic/solver.py:376-379`](../../../ein.py/src/ein/inference/monotonic/solver.py))
  says the opposite — *"Do NOT merge … extraction is UNSOUND under NAF
  (`absent`)"* — **and the solver is right**: with `h causes X` and
  `rule: absent X → Y`, the branch without `X` derives `Y` whose positive
  provenance never reaches `h`. Positive-edge DAG reachability
  (`_is_unconditional`'s test) cannot witness dependence-through-absence;
  a sound test needs `Deps(Y) = PositiveDeps(Y) ∪ NegativeDeps(Y)`.

The review: fully remove `CommitmentSetResult.unconditional_facts` +
`_is_unconditional` unless a *correct* application remains — don't keep a
conceptually dangerous dormant API.
