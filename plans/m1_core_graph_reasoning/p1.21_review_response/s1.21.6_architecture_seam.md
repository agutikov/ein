## The claim

The review proposes the core's target seam:

```text
ein-lang → typed IR → KB (ground atoms)
      ┌──────────────┴──────────────┐
 monotone closure            assumptions / worlds
 (Datalog-ish)                    lattice
      └──────────────┬──────────────┘
               complete model
                     │
               canonical key        ← S1.21.1's StateKey
                     │
            models / refutations
```

with **NAF sitting on the closure/world boundary** — not disguised as a
positive-premise variant inside the closure. Payoff: the M3 SMT integration
becomes much clearer (each side of the seam has an obvious SMT counterpart).

This stage *records* the seam and the gap between it and today's layout; it
does **not** move code.
