# S1h.20.2 — The plan that follows the survey

**Phase:** [P1h.20](README.md) of [M1h](../README.md)
**Status:** a place held, 2026-11-13. **It has no content, on purpose.** The milestone README: *A survey
first, and the phase's plan past the survey is written after the user has read it.* This file is where that
plan is written, by whoever runs [S1h.20.1](s1h.20.1_the_survey.md)'s stop, once the user has read the survey
and answered [Q-M1h.6](../open_questions.md#q-m1h6--whether-p1h20-and-p1h30-end-in-programs-at-all) for this
phase.
**Depends on:** the user's reading of the survey.

## What it rests on

The instruction, 2026-11-12: *"First survey methods of requirement verification."* The word is *first*: what
is second is not said, and nothing here guesses it.

## What is written here after the stop

By the user's answer:

- **If the phase goes on to programs,** in whole or in part: the stages that follow, as files of this
  directory numbered from S1h.20.3, each in the form the milestone's other stage files have: what it rests
  on, in the user's words; what it starts from; its tasks; its acceptance; what it must not do. This file
  becomes their index and says which of the survey's methods each takes. A stage that would ship a module,
  change the grammar or change what a surface prints begins with real programs whose answers the binary
  computed, shown to the user before anything ships.
- **If it does not:** this file records the answer in the user's words and what of the survey is kept (the
  page and the catalogue entries), and the phase is closed in the milestone's table as *a survey, read*.
  *"In part"* or *"no"* is a result.

## What it must not hold before then

No stage, task, relation, method chosen or program sketched. If something about what could follow is worth
keeping before the stop, it belongs in S1h.20.1's sixth task, on the survey's page, as a candidate.
