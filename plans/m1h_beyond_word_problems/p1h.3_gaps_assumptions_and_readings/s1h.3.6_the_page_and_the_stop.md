## Tasks

1. **T1h.3.6.1 — the page assembled.** The five sections in one reading order, every program re-run on the
   binary of the day, every number with its command and date; a figure an earlier stage took on another
   binary is re-taken or carries its own date.
2. **T1h.3.6.2 — the user's questions, answered in one place.** A section that sets each sentence of reply
   30 and of reply 33 beside the program that answers it, with a link to the stage's section. And round 2's
   two outlines, each marked *held* or *corrected*.
3. **T1h.3.6.3 — the proposals, each on real programs.** For each thing the banked problems hold one form
   of, what the comparison supports, shown as the banked problem rewritten in the proposed form with its
   read-out beside today's:
   - **the gap:** the derived fact stays, the debt replaces it, or both are offered, each a module;
   - **the assumption:** the marked fact stays, or the hypothesis;
   - **the model's boundary:** one fact, or one an assumption, as
     [S1h.3.3](s1h.3.3_which_assumptions_a_model_has_to_state.md) wrote it;
   - **universal assumptions:** a module or not, and where.
   A proposal the comparison does not support is not made: the page says *no change supported* and why.
4. **T1h.3.6.4 — the questions:**
   [Q-M1h.14](../open_questions.md#q-m1h14--which-of-the-banked-forms-change), which of the banked forms
   change, and [Q-M1h.15](../open_questions.md#q-m1h15--universal-assumptions-as-a-module), universal
   assumptions as a module; and any question the five stages raised, filed under the next free id first.
   What is **not** asked here, and where it is parked: open against false (M1g's P1g.7,
   [Q-M1g.13](../../m1g_logic/open_questions.md#q-m1g13--may-a-finished-search-call-an-unpayable-debt-dead));
   what a non-monotone program means ([F21](../../followups/f21_non_monotone_programs_decided.md)); a theory
   of solutions (M2).
5. **T1h.3.6.5 — the findings shown.** Every row of this phase's `findings.md`, each with its witness.
6. **T1h.3.6.6 — the stop.** The user reads the page. The rulings are recorded on it in the user's words and
   in [`open_questions.md`](../open_questions.md).
7. **T1h.3.6.7 — the re-plan.** [S1h.3.7](s1h.3.7_what_the_rulings_build.md) is restated to the rulings and
   given its tasks. If the user rules that nothing changes, that stage is restated to its close alone.

## Acceptance

- The page exists; every program on it was run by the binary; every number carries its command and date.
- Each sentence of replies 30 and 33 has its answer and its program on the page.
- The milestone's acceptance line is met: *the forms of a gap, of an assumption and of a reading each
  compared on the same problems*.
- The user has ruled, or has said which questions wait; the page and `open_questions.md` record it in the
  user's words.
- Nothing outside `plans/` and `build/` moved.

## What it must not do

- **Not change a banked problem or module.**
- **Not take a default** on a question the user has not answered.
- **Not put a later phase's question, or another milestone's, to the user here.** T4 says where each is
  parked.

## The standing rules

Questions go to [`open_questions.md`](../open_questions.md) under the next free id; findings to this phase's
`findings.md`. A ruling is recorded as the user gave it and may be reopened by a later finding. The link check
is run before the commit, which changes pages only.
