## Context

The phase README's row: *where in the corpus the third-kind reads are, by rule
family and by polarity, and on how many entries the cube observes a violation
(a dead node with an alive superset, a discharged node with an owing superset,
a hole)*. The README's work order puts this stage first, because every other
stage here is sized by it.

**What they leave open**, and what this stage answers:

- **The site, not the program.** Every count above is per program. The stages
  after this one need each site: which rule, written where (a stdlib module or
  the program), which `SiteKind`, at what `absent` depth, whether its rule
  fires at root's fixpoint (the fork-or-scratch question,
  [S1e2.1b.2](s1e2.1b.2_fork_or_scratch.md)), and whether it concludes a
  refutation (the learning question,
  [S1e2.1b.4](s1e2.1b.4_refutation_resting_on_absent.md)).
- **A violation, attributed to its site.** The cube counts violations per
  program. A minimal hole already names its `absent` guard
  (`region::Node::absent_premises`) or its owed instances, which is enough to
  tie each hole to the site that made it. Nothing does that today.
- **The conservative flags.** Region census § 8 names eight programs whose
  site the corpus never exercises. S1e2.1a.1 found five more flagged only
  because the **blind** rung proposes a structural relation that a stdlib or
  puzzle rule reads under `absent`: `features/05`, `features/12` and the three
  `square-unique` demos (`is-a`, `instance`, `next-to`). On `features/05` and
  `features/12` S1e2.4.1 showed that no candidate has the shape the `absent`
  reads: the blind generator proposes `(is-a Red House-1)`, never membership
  in a type. S1e2.4.1
  [§ 4](../p1e2.4_what_the_switch_left/s1e2.4.1_refutation_under_absent.md#what-each-answer-leaves-open)
  parked the consequence here: *whether the static check should be
  fact-level, not relation-level*.
- **The derived class.** S1e2.4.1's `via` column, 5 rules over 4 entries, and
  [`via_hole.ein`](../p1e2.4_what_the_switch_left/s1e2.4.1_probes/via_hole.ein),
  a dead hole whose refuting rule reads no `absent`.
- **The activator kind.** `SiteKind::Activator` has no fixture
  ([S1e2.1b.6](s1e2.1b.6_the_two_lattices_compared.md)'s acceptance asks for
  one).
- **Past the limit.** Eight flagged programs are past `--region-limit 16`:
  `lattice/06_friends_tables` (n = 19), `features/01`, `/04`, `/05`, `/12`
  (n = 20 for `/12`), and `square-unique/cul-de-sac`, `/corner-house`,
  `/terminus` ([`route1_reach.md` § 1](../p1e2.1a_the_solution_region/route1_reach.md#1-the-three-routes-over-the-corpus)).
  Their cube is not observed, so whether their sites do harm is not known.

## Acceptance

- **One census, one command, one selection, dated.** It extends
  [`refutation_census`](../../../ein.rs/crates/ein-infer/examples/refutation_census.rs)
  or is a sibling that shares its loader, and it copies no logic: it calls
  `monotone::classify` and `region::compute` on the same root fixpoint. The
  selection is `corpus_files()` and the register's witnesses, which is
  `refutation_census`'s, so the four counts above can be re-derived from its
  rows. S1e2.4.1's probes are a second run over
  `p1e2.4_what_the_switch_left/s1e2.4.1_probes/`, reported in a column of
  their own, because `plans/` is deleted when the milestone closes and no test
  may point there.
- **Per site:** the rule; where it is written (`file:line`, stdlib module or
  program); its `SiteKind`; the relation (`monotone::Rel`) and the `absent`
  depth; the activator; what the rule concludes (a positive fact, `(false)`,
  `(not …)`, `(open …)`); and whether it fires at root's fixpoint.
- **Per flagged program within the limit:** n; the cube's three violations,
  each counted with its smallest witness (`Topology`'s
  `dead_not_upward_closed`, `discharged_not_upward_closed`, `holes`); every
  minimal hole attributed to the sites its `absent_premises` or its owed
  instances name; and a verdict, **exercised** (a violation the cube observes)
  or **conservative** (a site, and no violation).
- **Per flagged program past the limit:** n, what route 2 certifies today
  ([`read_out.md` § 6.2](../p1e2.1a_the_solution_region/read_out.md#62-a-partial-run-c3)),
  and, for those at 17–19, the oracle's projected cost (`region::Projection`)
  if the limit were raised.
- **The grain, prototyped and tested.** For each site, whether a candidate,
  or a fact a saturation plan derives from candidates, can match the pattern
  the site reads. That is the static check at **fact grain**. A test holds the
  prototype to the cube: on no cube within the limit may the fact-grain
  verdict be *monotone* while the cube observes a violation. The census
  reports which programs would leave the flagged set. **Nothing ships**: the
  static check stays at relation grain until the user rules (below).
- **The four counts reconciled.** Each is re-derived from this census's rows
  under its own selection, and every difference is named: a file added since,
  a selection that differs, or a count that was wrong.
- **The page:** `site_census.md`, in this phase's directory, dated, with the
  command that re-takes it. S1e2.1b.6's *detection* row and
  its census numbers cite it.
- **The re-estimate.** S1e2.1b.2–.5 are re-estimated from the census, and the
  phase README's header with them. This README's header says they were
  specified before it.
- **No answer moves.** The stage adds an example, a test and a page.

150 cubes then reachable. The grain test is a cargo test in
`ein-infer/tests/region.rs` beside
`monotone_programs_show_no_violation_and_the_non_monotone_three_are_flagged`.

## The question it puts to the user

**At what grain does the static check read?** Today a read is *dynamic* when
its **relation** is in D. The census says how many programs are flagged only
because a relation is in D while no candidate, and nothing derived from one,
can match the read.

| option | what it is | what it changes |
|---|---|---|
| **(a) relation grain** | as shipped | nothing. The conservative programs stay flagged and, past n = 16, keep route 2's partial answer |
| **(b) fact grain** | a read is dynamic only when a candidate, or a fact derived from candidates, can match its pattern | which programs the read-out calls *not monotone*, and so which route answers them. Within the limit no R moves, since every route is held to the oracle. Past it, a program that leaves the set gets route 1 and an exact answer. The theorem has to be restated at that grain and held to the cube, which is the prototype's test |
| **(c) C1's reading first** | [Q-M1e.12](../../../docs/history/m1e_review_processing/open_questions.md#q-m1e12--the-blind-rung-is-untyped-and-a-model-binds-a-type-as-an-object) decides what the blind rung proposes. If it stops proposing a structural relation, some of these programs leave the set with no change to the check | the census says which of the five C1 would reach. C1 is [S1e2.2.10](../p1e2.2_wrong_answers/s1e2.2.10_types_as_objects.md)'s, in P1e2.2, the last phase of the 2026-11-02 order, so taking its reading here is a reorder, and the user's to make |

**No recommendation before the prototype has run.** (b) is worth having only
if its test holds on every cube, and the census is what says whether it does.

**Where the ruling is written:** `monotone.rs`'s module docs (the theorem at
its grain), [`solution_semantics.md` § 1.6](../../../docs/kernel/inference/solution_semantics.md#16-monotone),
[`region_dump.md`](../../../docs/kernel/inference/region_dump.md)'s
§ Monotonicity, S1e2.1b.6's *detection* row, and this file. If the ruling is
(b), building it is a task of this stage, re-estimated then. The goldens it
moves are named before they move: every `--json-summary` `monotonicity`
block, and every [`read_out_examples.md`](../p1e2.1a_the_solution_region/read_out_examples.md)
run of a program that leaves the set.

## Tasks

### Task T1e2.1b.1.1 — The sites and the cube

The census example: every site with its fields, every flagged cube's
violations attributed to sites, and the past-the-limit rows.

**Done 2026-11-02**, at `6ab13625`, release build, stdlib = the checkout:

```sh
cargo run --release --manifest-path ein.rs/Cargo.toml -p ein-infer --example site_census            # 6 s
cargo run --release --manifest-path ein.rs/Cargo.toml -p ein-infer --example site_census -- --json   # one row per file
```

[`site_census`](../../../ein.rs/crates/ein-infer/examples/site_census.rs) is
`refutation_census`'s sibling. The two share one loader,
[`examples/shared/census.rs`](../../../ein.rs/crates/ein-infer/examples/shared/census.rs):
the selection, the load, and root's fixpoint as `region::prepare` builds it,
ending in `monotone::classify`. `refutation_census` was moved onto it, and its
output is byte-identical before and after, in text and in `--json`. The cube
is `region::compute` on a second load of the same program, and the census
asserts that both loads give the same sites and the same universe. Over the
267 files: 204 load and saturate, and 22 are flagged. 14 of the 22 are within
the limit (6 exercised, 8 conservative) and 8 are past it. They have 32 sites:
27 `absent`, 4 obligation triggers, 1 activator. Every minimal hole and every
violation witness is attributed to at least one site. No monotone cube shows
a violation. The page with the tables is T1e2.1b.1.3's.

### Task T1e2.1b.1.2 — The grain prototype and its test

Fact grain, computed beside relation grain and never routed on. The test
against the cube.

**Done 2026-11-02.** The prototype is
[`examples/shared/fact_grain.rs`](../../../ein.rs/crates/ein-infer/examples/shared/fact_grain.rs).
It is example code, read by `site_census` and by the test and by no route, so
nothing ships. It closes the candidates over the saturation plans as **shapes**
(a fact with some arguments *any*) and calls a site's read dynamic only when
its pattern unifies with a shape of that closure. A plan fires in the closure
only through a read at **even** `absent` depth, because a fork only adds and
only those reads can turn a match from false to true. The module docs carry
the argument. The census prints each site's verdict at both grains, and what
C1 would do to the flag.

The test is two cargo tests in `ein-infer/tests/region.rs`, beside
`monotone_programs_show_no_violation_and_the_non_monotone_three_are_flagged`:

- `fact_grain_never_calls_a_violated_cube_monotone`. On every corpus and
  register cube within the limit, and on six programs written into the test,
  a fact-grain verdict of *monotone* never meets an observed violation. It
  **holds**: 155 corpus cubes are monotone at fact grain (the release rows of
  `site_census --json`), every one of them monotone at relation grain too,
  and the cube shows a violation on none of them.
- `fact_grain_on_programs_written_to_test_it`. The corpus has no program
  within the limit that leaves the set, so the six programs are what make the
  claim bite. Two leave and show no violation. Three stay flagged with a hole:
  a derived fact, a read at depth 2, and a stored negative fill the absence.
  One stays flagged because a commitment derives an activator, and the
  prototype marks its closure incomplete. If the closure follows only
  top-level reads, the depth-2 program is called monotone beside a dead hole.
  That mutation was run on the census (2026-11-02), and the census marked the
  program `UNSOUND`.

**What the prototype finds** (`site_census`, 2026-11-02): 2 of the 22
flagged programs leave the set at fact grain, `features/05` and
`features/12`. Both are past the limit. No program within the limit leaves,
and none is unsound. The evidence for the ruling is the page's
(T1e2.1b.1.3).

## Notes

- **What it does not do.** It decides nothing about what R is on a flagged
  program. That is S1e2.1b.2–.4. It rewrites no site: under the *forbid*
  ruling a refuting site is S1e2.2.5's to refuse, and the others are what a
  program still has after *forbid*.
- **The activator fixture.** If the census finds no corpus program with an
  activator site, S1e2.1b.6's acceptance already asks for a synthetic one
  under `examples/lattice/`. This stage writes nothing there, and names the
  gap on the page.
- **The `-y` abort** on `corpus/fuzz_findings/d3-unsat-core.ein`, taken
  2026-10-16 before the switch
  ([region census § 9](../p1e2.1a_the_solution_region/region_census.md#9-the-config-principle)),
  is listed against the site it comes from, for S1e2.1b.2, which owns it.
