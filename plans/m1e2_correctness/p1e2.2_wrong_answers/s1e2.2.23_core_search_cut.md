## The case

The witness is [`F2_core_cut.ein`](../inventory_2026-11-05/findings/F2_core_cut.ein), re-taken on the inventory's snapshot build at `cdcc3994` (2026-11-05):
- `(A k)` has 2⁸ = 256 environments of eight facts, each `pᵢ` from `xᵢ` or `yᵢ`. `(B k)` rests on `(y1 k) … (y8 k)` alone, and `(A k) (B k)` derives `(false)`.
- `ein solve` prints a ten-fact core, `(x1 k) (x2 k) (y1 k) … (y8 k)`, with no qualifier.
- The eight `y` facts alone derive `(false)` ([`F2_core_cut_control.ein`](../inventory_2026-11-05/findings/F2_core_cut_control.ein)).
- With five pairs instead of eight ([`F2_core_cut_5.ein`](../inventory_2026-11-05/findings/F2_core_cut_5.ein)), the search stays under its budget and finds the all-`y` core.
- Re-taken at `3db6187f`, after S1e2.2.21 (2026-11-05): `ein solve` still prints `(x1 k) (x2 k)` among the core's facts, and the five-pair control still prints the all-`y` core.

The mechanism at `cdcc3994`:
- The core search keeps 64 environments per label (`ExplanationBudget`).
- `region.rs:1122` and `:1268` call `explain::smallest_contradiction_frontier`, which discards `Explanation::exhausted` (`explain.rs:618–623`).
- So a core from a cut search reaches the read-out, `--json-summary` and `nogoods.json` with nothing to say it was cut.

[S1e2.2.11](s1e2.2.11_alt_cap_core.md) says the cut *"is reported (`Explanation::exhausted`)"*, and F22 assumes the same. Both hold only for a library caller. It is not F22's case, because no fact in the witness has more than 2 of the 32 alternatives the cap allows.

The promise this breaks is [Q-M1e2.11](../open_questions.md#q-m1e211--which-derivations-is-the-unsat-core-the-smallest-over-and-may-a-config-flag-change-it) (i)(b): the smallest frontier over every derivation the program records.
