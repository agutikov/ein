## The method

1. **Bank and re-take each witness** on the stage's HEAD, in release and in debug. A probe moves under `examples/ein-bugs/wrong-answers/` with a corpus entry. A corpus program is its own witness.
2. **File the rows.** Each row's id is in the family whose mechanism it is, where one names it (W8 for `features/07`'s rule order, W10 for a core past the cap), else the next free number at the stage's start. Each row is `open`, reviewed `deferred`, with its owner. Its `now` is what the engine answers. Its `right` gives each reading the followup lists, as a contested row does. W-6's row takes the cheapest of its five programs that `wrong_answers.rs` can run within the gate.
3. **W-15 and W-10.** Write a nine-line probe for each, at most a quarter of a day apiece. A probe that shows the defect is a row. Otherwise the item is named in its owner's page with what was tried, and the close names it from there.
4. **The pages:**
   - F21 and F22 each list the rows they own;
   - the milestone's acceptance names every deferred row;
   - the register's header gives the counts.

## Acceptance

- Every live W item in the inventory that has an owner is either a register row, `open` and deferred to that owner, or is named on the owner's page with why it has no witness.
- No answer moves. Goldens move only by banking.
