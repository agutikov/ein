## The case

Three shipped mechanisms assume that if `S` is dead, so is every superset of
`S`: the kill cache (`hypgen.rs:464`, `write_negated`), the singleton
writeback, and the width-1 no-good. Under `(absent …)` that is false. A
refutation that rests on `(absent (q A))` is lifted by committing `(q A)`.
[D4](../../../docs/history/m1e_review_processing/search_soundness_probes/d4_q_m1e9_upward_closure.md)
reproduced it and built the matrix.

| witness | settings | today | right |
|---|---|---|---|
| [`ein-bugs/naf-upward-closure.ein`](../../../examples/ein-bugs/naf-upward-closure.ein) | **default** | `Solution`, model `{(q A)}` (+`(not (p A))`) | `Solution`, model `{(p A), (q A)}` |
| the same, `-K` | lever | `{(p A),(q A)}`, right *by accident* (D4), and the file's `:expect` then **fails** | — |
| [`examples/ein-bugs/wrong-answers/naf-nowb.ein`](../../../examples/ein-bugs/wrong-answers/naf-nowb.ein): `:enable-singleton-writeback false`, `-L` | lever | `Contradiction k = 0`, exhausted, core `(p A)` | `Solution k = 1` |
