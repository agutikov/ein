## The case

Three shipped mechanisms assume that if `S` is dead, so is every superset of
`S`: the kill cache (`hypgen.rs:464`, `write_negated`), the singleton
writeback, and the width-1 no-good. Under `(absent …)` that is false. A
refutation that rests on `(absent (q A))` is lifted by committing `(q A)`.
[D4](../../../docs/history/m1e_review_processing/search_soundness_probes/d4_q_m1e9_upward_closure.md)
reproduced it and built the matrix.

| witness | settings | today | right |
|---|---|---|---|
| [`ein-bugs/naf-upward-closure.ein`](../../../examples/ein-bugs/naf-upward-closure.ein) | **default** | `Solution`, model `{(q A)}` (+`(not (p A))`) | `Solution`, model `{(p A), (q A)}` |
| the same, `-K` | lever | `{(p A),(q A)}`, right *by accident* (D4), and the file's `:expect` then **fails** | — |
| [`examples/ein-bugs/wrong-answers/naf-nowb.ein`](../../../examples/ein-bugs/wrong-answers/naf-nowb.ein): `:enable-singleton-writeback false`, `-L` | lever | `Contradiction k = 0`, exhausted, core `(p A)` | `Solution k = 1` |

**What shipped is containment, not a fix.** S1e.2.3 made
`(config :warn-derived-naf true)` narrate a `RefutationUnderAbsentWarning`
and measured the exposure: **9 rules over 7 corpus entries**, four of them
`std.elim`'s membership scans and none of them `connex` — 10 over 8 since
S1e2.1.1 banked `naf-nowb.ein`, the probe's twin. The wrong answer is
still there with the warning off, which is the default.

## The ruling comes first

There are two ways to make the answer right, and they are different languages:

| | what | consequence |
|---|---|---|
| **forbid** (S1f.10.8's option A) | a `(false)` or `(not …)` conclusion may not rest on an `(absent P)` whose relation is still extendable: a load error naming the replacement | the witness is refused, not answered. The 9 exposed rules over 7 entries must be rewritten or the program refused, and that is a stdlib change |
| **respect** | the three mechanisms stop learning from a refutation whose derivation reads an `absent` over an extendable relation: no kill-cache entry, no writeback, no width-1 no-good | the witness answers right; search gets slower exactly where the refutation was not monotone. F18's world-aware negatives are the general form, and this is the narrow one |

S1f.10.8 was written to take this ruling as *a corollary in
`absent_semantics.md`*, with its T1 narrowing the 60 syntactic sites to the
exposed set. That narrowing is half done (S1e.2.3's census). **Recommendation:
run S1f.10.8's T1 and its ruling here, as this stage's first task**, and
leave S1f.10.8 the page-writing. The user decides in the review.

## What moves

Under *forbid*: `std.elim` and the 7 entries, `examples/broken/load/` gains the
refusal, and `refutation_under_absent.rs` changes from a census to a refusal
test. Under *respect*: the witness's `:expect` (today's answer), and possibly
enterings on the 7 entries. Measure them: no model should move on any entry
except the witness. D4's matrix becomes a test, every configuration answering
`{(p A),(q A)}`.
