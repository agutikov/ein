## The case

`explain` explains each contradiction of a dead node by its witness alone and
keeps the smallest, and a pair contradiction's witness is its positive `X`
(`contradiction::Contradiction::witness`). The core is then `X`'s frontier
and leaves out the derivation of `(not X)`, which is where the commitment is.
Where `X` is root's, the core is one fact root holds.

```
$ ein solve examples/zebra2.ein --dump-answer DIR
  DIR/nogoods.json: set [(drink-loc Tea House-4)]  core [(drink-loc Milk House-3)]  refuted_by [total]
```

`(drink-loc Milk House-3)` is a clue. On `zebra2.ein`, 201 of the 366
members of N report a core that names none of their commitment (re-taken
2026-11-04 at `d51dc9a1`, `build/w21-check/`). S1e2.2.19's measurement at
`a41eb76e` (its § Notes, `namecheck.py` over its sweep) found 71 219 of
214 940 dead-node cores on programs whose root is alive naming none of their
commitment, on ten corpus programs: `branching/01`, `/02`, `/12`,
`zebra.ein` (86 of 458), `zebra2-hints` (73 of 73), `zebra2` and
`zebra2-obligations` (201 of 366 each), `zebra2-minus-15` and its
`-obligations` twin (693 of 1 495 each), and `tests/stdlib/bijection/06`.

## The method

1. **Re-take and measure first**, at the stage's HEAD.
   Bank a small witness under `examples/ein-bugs/wrong-answers/`: a pair
   whose positive side is root's and whose negative side the commitment
   derives. Prototype the default fix: **a pair's frontier is the union of
   its two sides' frontiers**, each the smallest over every derivation the
   program records (Q-M1e2.11 (i)(b)), following activators as Q-M1e2.12
   (c) rules; a node's core is still the smallest over its contradictions.
   Run the full sweep and diff every core a run reports (`nogoods.json`,
   `--json-summary`'s `region.core`, the read-out's core and `dead:` lines,
   `trace[answer]`, the oracle's), against HEAD.
   - Every pair core may grow: that is the fix. List each program's moved
     cores by count, and every core that **named its commitment before and
     does not after**, or that **a non-pair contradiction** decides: either
     is a finding, and the stage stops and reports with a recommended
     default.
   - `:record-alternative-justifications false` may change a dead node's
     core and nothing else (Q-M1e2.11 (ii)(b)); measure it as well.
2. **File W21** `open`: the banked witness, and each corpus program whose
   cores name none of their commitment at HEAD, one row each, `right` its
   cores under the fix (the register's schema `/8` states N's cores). A
   program whose register run costs too much for `wrong_answers.rs` is held
   by the corpus-wide test in 4 instead, and this file says which.
3. **The fix**, in `explain` or `contradiction`, whichever step 1 finds
   narrower, with tests that fail at HEAD in both profiles.
4. **The claim as a test.** A test over the corpus, in `cargo test`: every
   core a run reports for a dead node whose root is alive names a fact root
   does not hold, and from it, with root's plans, saturation derives
   `(false)`. This is the class-4 claim no test has held; it would have
   caught W19 and W21. If the sweep's cost puts it past the per-commit gate,
   it runs over the non-slow entries there and over the slow ones under
   `EIN_CORPUS_SLOW=1`.
5. **The pages:** `explain.rs`'s statement of what a pair's frontier is,
   `glossary.md`'s core entry, `inference/README.md` and the read-out's
   vocabulary where they say what a core names.

## Acceptance

- Every W21 row `fixed`.
- The corpus-wide test holds on every entry, and fails on the stage's HEAD.
- No core moves that step 1 did not list.
