## The case

[`solution_semantics.md` § 2](../../../docs/kernel/inference/solution_semantics.md)
defines a solution as a saturated, consistent state in which **every
remaining hypothesis is inconsistent with the state**. Operationally that is
a *maximal alive commitment*. The engine records a node only when
`hypgen::complete` holds (`hypgen.rs:971` at `71dd237`), which means *the
generator proposes nothing*. A candidate that dies in **two or more** firings
is still proposed, so a commitment whose every extension dies is not
`complete` and is not recorded. Layer n+1 proves every child dead, and the
information is dropped. **At layer 0** the loop simply `break`s
(`solve.rs:1821`, `if a_layer.is_empty()`) without asking whether root is
now a solution. `finalise` (`solve.rs:2879-2891`) then reports `Contradiction`
with the union of the dead cores, which are hypotheses and not program facts,
and `exhausted = !truncated` certifies it.

## The fix, as filed and as it has to be

[D3](../../m1e_review_processing/p1e.1_open_questions/s1e.1.1_search_soundness_probes/d3_q_m1e8_file_or_take.md)
sketched it: *record a surviving commitment whose every superset died*, at
the cost of one bitset over `a_prev` per layer, free under `-e`, and deferring
a model by one layer under `-n 1` (a trade-off to measure). Two things the
sketch does not cover:

1. **Layer 0.** Root is the commitment ∅. When every singleton dies, root is
   maximal and must be recorded, so the `break` at `solve.rs:1821` becomes a
   record. The sketch's bitset is over `a_prev` and never sees root.
2. **The state recorded is not the state that survived.** Its children's
   deaths **entail negatives**: if `S ∪ {h}` is dead, then `¬h` holds in `S`.
   Under ruling A those negatives are part of the state, and a rule may
   read them. So the record must first write them, re-saturate, and re-check
   (the same re-saturation `record_node` already does for `CO-M1`). Without it
   the fix would record states like `complete-records-stale`'s `{(q A)}` once
   its killing rule is made two-step. This is also W9's mechanism
   ([S1e2.2.9](s1e2.2.9_kill_cache_off_entailment.md)).

Root, when layer 1 is empty, through the same path. `forced-2-two` answers
`Solution`. `owed-two-step` answers `Open — owes 1`, because the recorded root
owes what nothing can pay, and that is the verdict the obligations arm already
gives the one-firing twin.

### Task T1e2.2.1.4 — The read-out of a `Contradiction`

After this stage, `Contradiction` with `exhausted = true` means *root itself is
dead*. That is the only way no maximal commitment exists. Check that every
remaining corpus `Contradiction` is that, and that `finalise`'s union of dead
cores is now never the evidence for one.
