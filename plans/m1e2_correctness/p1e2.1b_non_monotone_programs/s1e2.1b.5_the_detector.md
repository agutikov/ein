## (a) The explicit check

*Is this program monotone, and if not, where and why?* It answers a question
about the **program**, the third property of
[`monotonicity.md` § 7.6](../../../docs/kernel/inference/monotonicity.md#76-the-program-the-third-stronger-property),
and what the static check's sites say about the two node properties.

**Its CLI form is proposed on real corpus programs, with outputs, and
approved by the user before it is built** (T1e2.1b.5.1). The forms the
proposal drafts, none chosen here:

| form | what it is | why it gives no second answer | its work |
|---|---|---|---|
| **(a1) a mode of `ein solve`**, e.g. `--monotone` | `solve` answers a question about the program instead of describing R | `solve` stays the one tool. The mode prints what every bare run's `lattice` row and `monotonicity` block already carry, from the same function, and computes no node beyond root | load, root's fixpoint, `classify` and the static detector |
| **(a2) a subcommand**, e.g. `ein check FILE` | a sixth subcommand beside `saturate`, which also answers without a lattice | it computes no R, so it cannot disagree with `solve` about R; its verdict is `classify`'s, and a test holds it byte-equal to every surface's | the same |
| **(a3) a claim form**, e.g. in `(solve :expect …)` | a program states the verdict it expects, and `ein test` holds it; a refuted claim exits 1 | a claim checked against the same function | the static check |
| **(a4) `ein oracle`, as it is** | already prints what the cube saw, and exits 1 when a *Monotone* verdict is contradicted | — | 2ⁿ: more than the question needs, and nothing past the limit |

The forms are not exclusive. The proposal also asks whether the check reports
the cube's half (exercised or conservative) within the limit, at 2ⁿ, or leaves
it to `ein oracle`, and whether the optional dynamic check of (b) ships.

**The programs the proposal is drafted on**, each with its output, exit code
and wall: `examples/zebra.ein` (monotone, n = 56); `branching/13_lookahead_naf_world`
(conservative); one exercised program per cause, `naf-upward-closure` (an
`absent` that refutes), `lattice/08` (one that takes a witness away),
`lattice/04` (a trigger), `lattice/09` (an activator); `stale-naf-fork` (fork
≠ scratch); `features/07` (an even cycle, rule-order sensitive);
`d3-unsat-core` (an odd cycle); S1e2.1b.7's FIFO-gap fixture; and two past the
limit, `lattice/06_friends_tables` and `features/04_open`. Outputs come from
the binary where a form's output exists and from the census examples where it
does not, marked *drafted*.

## (b) The detector in a bare `ein solve`

Every check below runs only on a program `classify` flags. **On an unflagged
program the detector adds no work**: a program with no site has no `absent`
over D, so its graph over D has no negative edge, no equal-priority pair can
race, no fact can be stale, and no path can differ (the theorem of
[`monotonicity.md` § 5.1](../../../docs/kernel/inference/monotonicity.md#51-static)).
That argument goes beside the code, under
[`standard_of_proof.md`](../../../docs/kernel/standard_of_proof.md)'s rule 2,
and T1e2.1b.5.6 measures it.

**Static, at load:**

| check | what it reports | from |
|---|---|---|
| **sites by kind** | each site: rule, kind (`absent`, trigger, activator), relation, depth, written where | exists: `monotone::classify` |
| **cycles through an `absent`** | the rule dependency graph over D; each strongly connected component with a negative edge, with its parity (odd: a stable model need not exist; even: there can be several) | `node_census -- --strata`, moved into the library. Over D it flags `features/07`, `f07-fpoff` and `d3-unsat-core` (node_census § 5) |
| **order sensitivity** | two `absent` rules at one priority where one reads, under `absent`, what the other concludes: the boundary admits them in written order, so the state can depend on the text (the FIFO gap, and `features/07`'s even cycle) | new. Held to node_census § 7: it must flag every program whose R or universe moves under an equal-priority reorder (`features/07`, `f07-fpoff`, the written `stratified_stale`) and S1e2.1b.7's FIFO fixture |

**Dynamic, cheap:**

| check | what it reports | cost |
|---|---|---|
| **the cube's violations** | within the limit: holes, dead nodes with an alive superset, discharged nodes with an owing superset, each attributed to its site (S1e2.1b.3's rule). **Exercised** or **conservative**. Past the limit: *not known* | exists: route 2's `Topology`. Attribution: S1e2.1b.3's |
| **stale facts in the listed states** | for each state the run lists, each fact whose primary derivation has an `absent` guard the state's own fixpoint falsifies, re-judged with the engine's matcher | one matcher call per fact with an `absent` premise, per listed state: priced in T1e2.1b.5.6 |
| **a parent-path spot check** (optional) | for each listed state of k ≥ 2 commitments, up to a bounded number of its parents C \ {h}: does the parent's fixpoint plus h reach the same state? | one saturation per parent checked. Ships only if T1e2.1b.5.1's proposal is approved with its price |

**The warnings.** On every surface that reports R. Each says three things,
in words the proposal shows first: **what is assumed** (a node is built by
fork from root, and what a node's state should be is open: Q-M1e2.2, F21);
**which property is at stake** (the state's or the status's, per site, from
S1e2.1b.7's taxonomy); and **whether it bit** (exercised, with what the cube
or a stale fact showed; conservative; or not known past the limit).

| surface | today | becomes |
|---|---|---|
| the read-out's `lattice` row | *not monotone: … assumes a monotone lattice for what a node is (P1e2.1b)* | the warning, under the 100-line cap; every site and every stale fact in `--dump-answer` |
| `--json-summary`'s `monotonicity` block | sites and reads | plus cycles, order sensitivity, stale facts, *exercised* / *conservative* / *not known*, and what is assumed |
| `ein test` and `--json-report` | `monotone` per row; the claim check's *not monotone* line | the warning beside each claim on a flagged program; the report's row gains the warning's fields |
| `--dump-answer` | the read-out uncut | the warning whole |
| the event stream | the `monotonicity` event, *a stub* | the warning's fields; `events.md` says so |
| `ein oracle` and `--dump` | *(assumes a monotone lattice)*, `assumes_monotone` | reworded; the field kept or renamed, named first |
| `ein render region` | *the lines assume a monotone lattice (P1e2.1b)* | reworded |
| the review bundle | *not monotone* | the warning, and a column for exercised, conservative or not known |
| the register | the W8 rows' `open_because` and every flagged row's qualifier: *"… P1e2.1b's"* | re-pointed to F21, held by `wrong_answers.rs` |

## (c) Robust

Held by tests, in the gate:

1. **A *Monotone* verdict implies no cube violation**, on every corpus and
   register cube within the limit (extends
   `region.rs::monotone_programs_show_no_violation_and_the_non_monotone_three_are_flagged`)
   and on the programs `utils/region_synth.py --random` writes, as
   `region_dump.rs::a_random_region_written_as_a_program_comes_back` already
   draws them per commit.
2. **A *Monotone* verdict implies one state per node, whatever built it**: by
   fork, from scratch and by every split C1 ⊆ C, on every fact of every node,
   on the corpus cubes within a bound on n the stage states with its cost, and
   on the random programs. `node_census -- --control` is the fork-against-
   scratch half today. A split forks from a fixpoint that already holds
   root's, so it cannot race over a static read; scratch can, and if
   S1e2.1b.7's static variant shows that it does, the test names that case as
   the one where scratch departs from the verdict.
3. **No dynamic finding on an unflagged program**: no stale fact and no path
   difference, on the same sweep. That is (2)'s contrapositive, asserted
   directly.
4. **Order sensitivity has no false negative** on node_census § 7's rows and
   S1e2.1b.7's FIFO fixture.
5. **One verdict**: the explicit check's verdict and sites are byte-equal to
   the `lattice` row's, the summary's block and `ein test`'s row, on every
   corpus program.

**Overhead, measured corpus-wide**: per entry, the `work` row and wall before
and after; identical work and wall within noise on every unflagged entry; the
added cost on each flagged one, by check. `utils/corpus_cost.py` re-takes the
`slow` column, and no entry may cross the 1 s line because of the detector.
