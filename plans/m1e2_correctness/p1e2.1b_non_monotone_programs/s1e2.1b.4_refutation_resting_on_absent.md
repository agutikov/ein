## Context

**The ruling it applies.** 2026-11-02, the user, after the
[explainer](../p1e2.4_what_the_switch_left/s1e2.4.1_the_question_explained.md):
*"That is exactly what I meant (absent q ?x) => false - this is incorrect form
of obligation, isn't it?"*, then *"forbid, and bank dual_rail.ein in the
docs"*. Over a relation a commitment can still extend, `(absent P)` ⟹
`(false)` or `(not …)` is refused at load, with the error naming a form that
says what was meant
([S1e2.4.1 § 5](../p1e2.4_what_the_switch_left/s1e2.4.1_refutation_under_absent.md#5-the-ruling)).
F1–F5 were left to [S1e2.2.5](../p1e2.2_wrong_answers/s1e2.2.5_naf_upward_closure.md),
which builds the refusal.

**The phase README's row since that ruling:** *"Once S1e2.2.5's refusal ships,
a program the engine loads refutes from no extendable absence, so what is left
here is the derived case if S1e2.2.5's F2 leaves it out, and what may learn
before the refusal ships."*

**Before the refusal ships is a long time.** S1e2.2.5 is in P1e2.2, and the
user's order of 2026-11-02 runs P1e2.1b, P1e2.1c, P1e2.3, the revisit, and
P1e2.2 last ([milestone README § Phases](../README.md#phases)). So every
program S1e2.4.1 counted as exposed (13 rules over 9 entries under the static
check's reading) still loads while P1e2.1c builds the region search.

**What learns today** ([S1e2.4.1 § 2.2](../p1e2.4_what_the_switch_left/s1e2.4.1_refutation_under_absent.md#22-under-respect)):
no shipped mode learns from such a refutation. Route 1 learns by L1, and only
on a monotone program. Route 2, the cube, learns nothing. L0 gives root's
death to every node on `features/12`, the one corpus entry whose root such a
rule kills. The walk's kill cache, singleton writeback, width-1 no-good and
`apriori` filter have no CLI path. **So the question binds work not done yet**:
P1e2.1c's routes, and the guard split's cells in particular.

**What the engine already has.** `region::Node::absent_premises` lists the
`absent` premises anywhere in the primary derivation of a dead node's
contradiction, root's facts included. It is a per-refutation taint, S1e2.4.1's
*R1, per refutation* grain, computed by the oracle and read by nothing that
learns.

**An overlap, named.** The phase README's inherited row says this stage
*"also decides whether S1e2.1a.1's detector replaces"* the
`RefutationUnderAbsentWarning`. Under *forbid* the warning becomes the
refusal's text (S1e2.2.5's T1e2.2.5.3), and which reading of *extendable* the
refusal uses, *eligible* (S1e.2.3's) or *in D* (the static check's), is F1,
S1e2.2.5's. So this stage hands S1e2.2.5 the evidence for F1 and does not
decide it.

## The options, for F21's D6

**Not asked in this stage** since 2026-11-02. The question and its options
stay here as the evidence is gathered against them, and the decision is
F21's D6:

> **Until S1e2.2.5's refusal ships, and afterwards for whatever F2 leaves
> loading, may a region route carry a refutation past the node whose own
> fixpoint derived it, as a skipped superset, a stored `(not h)` or a clause,
> when that refutation rests on an `absent` over a relation in D?**

| option | what may learn | what it costs or changes |
|---|---|---|
| **(a) per program** | no route learns on a program the static check flags. Learning is L1, on monotone programs only. This is what ships | a flagged program gets no licence but the cube, so past `--region-limit` it is answered in part, as `features/05` is today ([S1e2.4.1 § 2.2](../p1e2.4_what_the_switch_left/s1e2.4.1_refutation_under_absent.md#22-under-respect)). The guard split's cells could not learn either |
| **(b) per refutation** | a route may learn from a dead node whose contradiction's derivation has no `absent` premise over D, directly or through a derived relation, read off provenance. This is [F18](../../followups/f18_world_aware_negatives.md)'s narrow form, D4's option C, S1e2.4.1's R1 | a licence held to the oracle, below. It moves no answer by construction, and it is what would let a search past the limit (F21's D7) learn inside a flagged program |
| **(c) the refusal first** | bring S1e2.2.5's build of the refusal into P1e2.1b, F1–F5 with it, so that by the time P1e2.1c learns, nothing exposed loads | a reorder of the user's 2026-11-02 order, so it is the user's to make. It front-loads the stdlib and corpus change S1e2.4.1 priced at 9 to 12 entries. If F2 leaves `via` out, `via_hole.ein`'s dead hole still loads and (a) or (b) must cover it |

**The derived case**, whichever option: S1e2.4.1's `via` set
([§ 1](../p1e2.4_what_the_switch_left/s1e2.4.1_refutation_under_absent.md#the-via-set))
and [`via_hole.ein`](../p1e2.4_what_the_switch_left/s1e2.4.1_probes/via_hole.ein).
(b) covers it if its taint reads the whole derivation, which
`absent_premises` does.

**A recommendation, mine, recorded for F21 and not a ruling, conditional on
the test below:** (b) if the oracle check holds on every flagged cube, and (a)
if it does not. (b) changes no
answer and is the licence a search past the limit (F21's D7) would need. (c) is a different trade
the user may prefer, since it makes the guarantee a language one.

## How it is measured

1. **The taint census.** Every dead node on every flagged cube S1e2.1b.1
   lists, plus `via_hole.ein` and the register's W3 and W8 witnesses: its
   `absent_premises`, each classified as static, in D directly, or in D
   through a derived relation. Counted: tainted and untainted deaths, and for
   each whether it has an alive superset.
2. **The check, as a test.** An untainted dead node with an alive superset is
   a counterexample to (b), and every dead hole is tainted. A cargo test in
   `ein-infer/tests/region.rs` asserts both on every flagged cube within the
   limit. It is a property of the oracle's provenance, cheap, and it fails if
   (b) stops being sound.
3. **What (b) buys over (a).** On the flagged cubes, the supersets an
   untainted death lets a route skip, counted, beside the cube's 2ⁿ.
4. **The two readings of *extendable*** on the same refutations, *eligible*
   and *in D*, handed to S1e2.2.5 for F1. S1e2.4.1 § 1 has them per rule. This
   adds them per dead node.
5. **W8's `p-and-q-clash` under each of S1e2.1b.2's options.** Under fork it
   is a refutation through `(p one)`, which root derived under `(absent (q
   one))`. Under scratch it does not fire on `{(q one)}`.
6. **The refutation a node compiles.** `region.rs`'s `ACTIVATOR_REFUTES`: an
   activator compiles `rule watch`, which reads `(absent (?R ?x))`, at a node,
   and its only site is the activator (S1e2.1b.6). A load-time refusal that
   reads root's plans cannot see it, which is S1e2.2.5's F3. The census counts
   how many corpus programs have an activator in D whose rule concludes a
   refutation (the site census found one activator site in the corpus,
   `features/01`'s, never exercised), and states what F3's two placements, at
   load and at each node's compile, would each catch on the corpus and on the
   written program.

The page is a section of S1e2.1b.1's `site_census.md`, or a page of its own if
it outgrows one, dated, with the commands.

test fails there, which was run and reverted. 0.1 s in a release or a debug
build.

- **Owing holes are not refutations.** A debt a commitment creates is not
  learned from by any route, and pruning across an owing hole (L5's *owing
  meets dead*) is a monotone licence. What a search may conclude across a
  trigger is F21's D4 and D7, with [S1e2.1b.3](s1e2.1b.3_holes.md)'s
  evidence.
- **The walk's learners** are not this stage's to rule on. They have no CLI
