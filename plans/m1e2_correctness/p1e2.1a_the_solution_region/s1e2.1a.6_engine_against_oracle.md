**Status:** done 2026-11-01, all three tasks.

## Context

Every right answer in the register was derived by hand until S1e2.1a.10.
M1e's record says it *did not check the engine's answers*, and M1e2 exists
because of that. For programs whose candidate list is small, the oracle turns
*the right answer* from a reading into a computation. The user's config
principle makes *under every lever* a correctness claim rather than a cost
measurement.

Two of the three halves this stage had are done elsewhere:

- **The modes equal the oracle**, at default levers, on every program it
  enumerates: `ein-infer/tests/explore.rs` (T1e2.1a.9.4), with C1–C3, and C4
  for `-j`.
- **The walk against R**, every declared run and lever cell, is
  S1e2.1a.10's census. It has to run before the switch, because after the
  switch nothing runs the walk.

What remains is **C4 under every lever**. After the switch `-L`, `-K` and `-y`
stay as levers inside the modes
([`solve_interface.md` § 6](solve_interface.md#6-what-moves)), and nothing yet
holds a mode's answer under one of them to the default's.

## Acceptance

- **Lever invariance, as a test.** For every corpus entry and register witness
  whose R is known, and every `levers` cell it declares, each mode's answer
  under the lever equals its answer at the default. That covers the states
  `-n` prints, which C4 requires. It is the config principle as a test.
  It runs in the gate if it is cheap enough and in the nightly otherwise, with
  the cost measured and written down.
- **Every disagreement is a register row** or an explained non-defect: a
  budget that cut the run and says so. New rows get ids after W12, with the
  oracle's answer as `right` and `derived = "the oracle, …"`. A new W-case
  gets a P1e2.2 stage or a written reason it does not.
- **R under every lever.** `utils/region_census.py --levers` is re-taken after
  the switch, and the one flag that moves R today,
  **`enable-symmetric-mirror`** on `features/06_symmetric_native.ein`, is filed
  as a register row. **Ruled 2026-11-01: retire the flag.** The other option,
  making it switch the mirror's implementation and not its meaning, was not
  taken. The stage retires it as S1e2.1a.5 retired nine fields: a load error
  that says why, the `configuration.md` row and census (8 settable flags of 17
  fields becomes 7 of 17), and `utils/feature_matrix.py`'s
  `no-symmetric-mirror` cell, a walk instrument. A program that wants a
  relation left unmirrored does not write `(__symmetric__ R)`. No corpus
  program sets the flag. The register row is fixed in the commit that retires
  it.

### Task T1e2.1a.6.1 — The lever cells, after the switch

**Done 2026-11-01**, read at `cafac4de`.

**The cells.** `corpus/corpus.toml` declares **30** lever cells on **12**
entries, each a `solve <lever>` run (`ein_corpus::Entry::all_runs`), which
since the switch is a bare describe:

- `-L`, `-K` and `-y` on eight: `branching/04_two_levels`,
  `branching/05_mini_zebra`, `domain_elim/ab`, `lattice/01_subset_pruned`,

- `-L` and `-K` on two: `branching/13_lookahead_naf_world` and
  `branching/14_lookahead_unjudgeable`;
- `-L` on two: `ein-bugs/wrong-answers/forced-1` and `naf-nowb`.

The switch took the eight `-o score-sum` cells. **The register adds no
cell.** Five rows name a CLI lever in `settings`, all `-L`: W1/lattice-02-L,
W1/branching-04-L, W1/lattice-01-L, W2/forced-1-L and W3/naf-nowb-L. Each
witness already declares it. Five rows name a lever set **in the file**:
W1/fresh-name-nospawn, W1/branching-07 and W4/alive-set-fresh-name (the
lookahead off), W1/forced-2-off (the forced-positive cascade, retired at the
switch, so the program is its sibling's) and W3/naf-nowb-L (the singleton
writeback, the same). A lever in the file is part of the program, which is
T1e2.1a.6.2's in-process sweep's subject. No row's `[case.now]` carries a
lever since the switch.

**What `-L`, `-K` and `-y` do inside `ein_infer::explore`: nothing, and
nothing can.** [`explore::Options`](../../../ein.rs/crates/ein-infer/src/explore.rs)
holds a mode, a budget, a limit, `jobs` and a deadline, and no config. The
CLI writes the three shadows into `solve::resolved_config`'s copy of the
program's config. That copy is what `-c` prints, what the `run` event carries
and what the summary echoes as `config`, and it never reaches the KB
([`modes.rs`](../../../ein.rs/crates/ein-cli/src/modes.rs)). A route reads
the **program's** config, through the KB, in three places only:

- the saturator, which every node of every route runs, reads
  `enable-symmetric-mirror` and `record-alternative-justifications`;
- `region::prepare_with` reads `warn-derived-naf`, for root's two `warn`
  events;
- `hypgen::generate_config_free`, which builds H, pins the lookahead and the
  kill cache off and the obligations rung to `rule-order`, whatever the
  config and `EIN_OBLIGATION_CHOICE` say.

The other five flags (the lookahead, the kill cache, path nogoods, the
fail-fast fork, the sanity check) are read by the walk's library alone
(`solve.rs`, `commitment.rs`, `hypgen::generate`), and so are
`EIN_OBLIGATION_CHOICE`, `EIN_TRAVERSAL` and `EIN_BATCH_PER_WORKER`. One
surface outside the routes still runs that library, the summary's `root`
block; T1e2.1a.6.2's test found it.

**Which lever is meaningful for which mode:**

| lever | how it reaches a run | what reads it | modes it can move | today |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `-L`, `-K`, `-y` | the CLI, into the resolved config's copy | `-c`, the `run` event, the summary's `config` echo | none | inert by construction: P1e2.1c's speed levers ([`solve_interface.md` § 2.4](solve_interface.md#24-the-config--fields)) |
| `(config …)`: the lookahead, the kill cache, path nogoods, the fail-fast fork, `lattice-sanity-check` | the program, into `kb.program().config` | the walk's library; the summary's `root` block (the first two) | none | read by no route |

| `record-alternative-justifications` | the program, into the saturator's provenance | which core a dead node names | the cores printed: an empty R's unsat core, N's in `--trace` and `nogoods.json` | explanation only, by design (`configuration.md` § 2.2) |
| `warn-derived-naf` | the program, into root's narration | two `warn` events | none printed | the event stream only |
| `EIN_OBLIGATION_CHOICE`, `EIN_TRAVERSAL`, `EIN_BATCH_PER_WORKER` | the environment | the walk; the summary's `root.hypgen` (the first) | none | read by no route |
| `-j` | the CLI, into `explore::Options::jobs` | every route's fan-out | every mode | C4 held already: `explore.rs`, `region_modes.rs`, `jobs_invariance.rs` |
| `--region-budget`, `--region-limit`, `-T`, `-n` | the CLI | where a run stops, or what it asks | — | questions and budgets, not levers: C3 |

### Task T1e2.1a.6.2 — The test, and where it runs

**Done 2026-11-01**:
[`ein-cli/tests/lever_invariance.rs`](../../../ein.rs/crates/ein-cli/tests/lever_invariance.rs),
two tests, one per route a lever takes into a run. Both ask each of `ein
solve`'s **fifteen questions**: all of R, the minimal line and the maximal
line, each × describe, `-n 1`, `-n 2`, `-n k_s+1` and `--single`. Where
`k_s+1` is 2 the question is asked once, so a program with one state gets
twelve. `-n k_s+1` is the run that lists every state, so the states `-n`
prints are compared as C4 requires.

- **`every_declared_lever_cell_moves_no_line_of_any_mode`**, through the
  binary. Every `levers` cell, and every CLI lever a register row's
  `settings` names, on the entries outside the slow tier: 10 files, R known
  on all ten. The exit code, stdout, stderr and the `--json-summary` are
  compared byte for byte with the same question at the default. The summary's
  `config` echo is left out and checked to carry the lever, so a lever that
  stopped reaching the run cannot pass. The walk's three `EIN_*` levers
  (`EIN_OBLIGATION_CHOICE=off|fail-first`, `EIN_TRAVERSAL=tree`) reach a run
  the same way, nothing declares them, and they are swept over the same
  files. **348 declared cells and 432 environment cells.**
- **`no_config_flag_moves_a_line_of_any_mode`**, in-process. Every flag a
  `(config …)` can set, flipped from the program's own resolved value in an
  appended block (the loader keeps the last block whole), on every corpus
  program and register witness outside the slow tier whose bare describe
  exhausts at the default budget. It compares the read-out rendered uncut,
  its stderr lines, its exit code and the summary. **180 programs and
  18 816 cells**; of the 255 files, 12 stop at the default budget and 63 have
  no read-out (load, parse and compile negatives). This sweep reaches more
  than the oracle does (169 programs at n ≤ 16), because route 1 knows R past
  the limit. The CLI shadows and the environment levers are swept on the ten
  declared files only. Neither reaches a route on any file, and the process
  path they take is the same for every file.

**The cost, and where it runs.** `cargo test -p ein-cli --test
lever_invariance`, 2026-11-01, this machine (32 cores, the test's eight
workers), dev profile: **14 s of wall clock and 114 s of CPU**. The in-process
sweep is all but 0.4 s of it, and `branching/06` and `/07` (n = 204, 74
states, 18 622 commitments) are half its CPU. In the same `cargo test
--workspace --no-fail-fast` run (6 min 43 s, 2026-11-01), it was the ninth
slowest target, beside `summary_properties` (14.2 s) and `search_invariants`
(13.0 s). **It runs per commit**, in `cargo test --workspace`.

The slow tier's cells, the `zebra2` pair, add 72 declared and 72
environment cells. They cost **77 s on eight workers and 674 s of CPU** in
release (`EIN_CORPUS_SLOW=1 cargo test -p ein-cli --release --test
lever_invariance`, 2026-11-01), so **they run in the nightly**, a new step
beside `answer_dump`'s. Their R is not known within the default budget, so
what they check is C3 and C4 together: a cut answer is the same cut answer
under every lever.

**What it found:**

1. **`enable-symmetric-mirror` on `features/06_symmetric_native.ein`**, on
   every one of its twelve questions. Off, root's fixpoint loses the mirror's
   three facts, and the query's one class says *the goal has no row* where
   `(knows Bob Ann)` holds. This moves R. It is the one lever that does, the
   one disagreement that is a wrong answer, and T1e2.1a.6.3's. Until then the
   test pins it in `KNOWN`, as a disagreement that must stay one on every
   question.
2. **The summary's `root` block**, found when the in-process sweep was
   widened from the read-out to the summary. `summary::root_block` runs the
   walk's `hypgen::generate` on a fork of root and counts root's facts after
   it. So `root.facts`, `root.facts_by_relation` and `root.hypgen` move under
   the program's lookahead and kill-cache flags on 19 programs, while `-L`
   and `-K` leave them alone. On `branching/02_one_dead_one_alive.ein` the
   block says 27 facts where root's fixpoint holds 23 (four kill-cache
   `(not h)` writes) and 30 candidates where H holds 48.
   `EIN_OBLIGATION_CHOICE=off` moves `root.hypgen` on `owed-seats.ein`.
   **Not a register row**: no answer moves. The read-out, every state and
   class, and every other block are identical. It is surface **S4** of the
   proposed P1e2.3, which this stage did not create and does not own.
   [The M1e2 README](../README.md#p1e23--surfaces-that-misreport-the-answer-proposed)
   now carries the re-finding beside S4's row. The test leaves the three
   fields out and its module note says why.
3. Nothing else, in any process cell or in any other (program, flag,
   question) cell.

**A claim this makes true.** `configuration.md` § 2.3 said the corpus's
`levers` cells *hold the read-out unmoved*. `corpus_cli.rs` checked their
exit codes only, so until this test the claim was untrue. The page now
cites the test (§ 2.3, § 2.5), and `corpus/README.md` § Levers no longer
describes `-o score-sum` and the walk's `-L`. § 4's *setting one changes
nothing an `ein` run prints* gained its one exception, S4.

### Task T1e2.1a.6.3 — The mirror row

**Done 2026-11-01, in one commit**: the flag retired, the row filed fixed, and
the goldens below moved.

**The retirement**, as S1e2.1a.5 retired nine:

- `ein_core::config::FLAGS` loses `enable-symmetric-mirror`, so a program can
  set **7** flags, and `RETIRED` gains it. Each entry of `RETIRED` now names
  its stage, so the load error says *"config flag :enable-symmetric-mirror
  was retired at M1e2 S1e2.1a.6: off, it changed the answer, and no config
  option may. The mirror is what `(__symmetric__ R)` means, so it is always
  on: a relation that is not to be mirrored is not marked"*. The nine keep
  S1e2.1a.5. `FIELDS` stays at 17.
- **The mirror's behaviour stays, unconditionally.** The saturator's
  `mirror_enabled` is gone, and a marked relation is always closed under
  arg-swap. The field `SolverConfig::enable_symmetric_mirror` stays at `true`
  and nothing reads it.
- **`.einb`**: META round-trips all 17 fields, so the format does not move,
  and nothing reads META's config. A container stores its program as text,
  and `sections::load_program` re-loads it through `ein_ir::load_query` on
  open, so a container saved earlier whose program sets the flag is refused
  as the `.ein` is.
- The echoes lose it: `--dump-config`, the `run` event's `config` and the
  summary's `config` print seven flags (`printers.rs`, `solve.rs`,
  `summary.rs`, `summary_properties.rs`'s key set).
- The instruments. `utils/fuzz_engine.py` no longer generates the flag, and
  `utils/region_census.py` no longer flips it, since a program setting it is a
  load error. `ein-infer`'s `lever_matrix` example drops its cell: the field
  is read by nothing, so the cell would measure nothing.
  `utils/feature_matrix.py`'s `no-symmetric-mirror` cell stays, with a
  comment, as the four cells S1e2.1a.5 retired stayed. The whole matrix runs
  only against a binary at or before `7ec8a480`, which reaches the flag.
  `features.md`'s rows are that record.
- `lever_invariance.rs` loses its `KNOWN` pin. With seven flags the
  in-process sweep is 16 464 cells on the same 180 programs, 12 s, and
  nothing moves.

**The row**, `W13/symmetric-mirror-off`, filed `fixed`, after W12. Its
witness is `features/06_symmetric_native.ein` itself, and no new file. The
witness after a retirement would be features/06's program anyway, as
`forced-2-off`'s became its sibling's at the switch.

- `wrong` is a **region run's** answer, the first row whose `wrong` is not the

- `right` is the oracle's: R = {∅}, with `(knows Bob Ann)` in root's

- `now` is a bare `solve`. The register's `[case.now]` cannot see the

  `compile_semantics.rs`), and the mirror's three firings in `ein-infer`'s
