## Context

The review paused after W1 because the definition changed under it. It resumes
when the bundle shows each case **in R's terms**, with the region drawn, and
when every register row's right answer has been restated under the new
definition.

## Acceptance

- **A § *The region* on every case page**, between *the answers* and *where it
  went wrong*. It holds:
  - S1e2.1a.2's drawing with the walk overlaid;
  - the oracle's lines beside the register's `right` and the engine's answer;
  - the missing and spurious nodes, linked to their per-node rows.

  Where n exceeds the limit, it holds one line saying so and the walk alone.
  `review_bundle.py --corpus` gets the same section.
- **The register restated.** Every `right` block is re-derived under the new
  definition. Where n ≤ 16 the oracle computes it, and `derived` says so. Where
  n > 16 it stays a hand derivation, and `derived` says that too. A right
  answer that changes is listed in this file with its reason.
- **W1, re-read in R's terms.** Its seven rows were confirmed on 2026-10-15
  before the definition was written. If the restated right answers still
  agree, the confirmation stands and the rows say so. If one does not, it
  goes back to the user.
  [S1e2.2.1](../p1e2.2_wrong_answers/s1e2.2.1_maximal_not_recorded.md) is
  restated against the maximal line.
- **The review resumes** on a bundle built at a recorded commit, which is
  written into the register's header. The twelve unread cases are read in
  P1e2.2's order.
