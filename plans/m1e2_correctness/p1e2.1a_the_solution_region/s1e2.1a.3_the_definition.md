## Context

[Q-M1e2.7](../open_questions.md#q-m1e27--the-answer-is-a-region-of-the-lattice)
gives the reading and leaves eight sub-questions, (a)–(h). Each is easier to
rule with the corpus measured than argued from five hand-picked programs.
This milestone's order is *see, then rule*, and this stage applies it to the
definition itself.

## Acceptance

- **The census**, `utils/region_census.py`: every corpus entry and register
  witness that declares a `solve` run, through `--dump-region`. It reports:
  - **n**: how many entries the oracle reaches at the default limit, and how
    many need it raised;
  - **|R|**, the size of each line, and whether the lines meet;
  - **holes**: which entries have them, and through which `absent`;
  - **the trivial line**: the obligations-only programs whose maximal line is
    *everything*;
  - **commitments against states**: where two commitments give one state;
  - **the universe**: where the config-free candidate list differs from
    `alive₀`.

  It is written as a dated page beside the census pages M1d banked, and it is
  re-takable.
- **The user's rulings** on (a)–(h), written into Q-M1e2.7 and dated. The
  stage puts the census on the table and a recommendation under each
  sub-question, and records the rulings. It argues only where a ruling would
  contradict a measured fact.
- **[`solution_semantics.md`](../../../docs/kernel/inference/solution_semantics.md)
  rewritten around R:**
  - the node classification;
  - R, its two lines, and holes;
  - what each verdict word, `k` and `exhausted` mean;
  - empty R as `Contradiction` or `Open`;
  - commitments against states;
  - the universe.

  The worked example (`lattice/02`) is redone as a drawing from S1e2.1a.2.
  § 6, *where the engine differs*, is rewritten against the new definition.
  [Q-M1e.6](../../../docs/history/m1e_review_processing/open_questions.md#q-m1e6--what-is-a-solution-and-what-is-a-model)
  gets a one-line forward pointer to Q-M1e2.7, and nothing else in M1e's
  record changes.
- **The config principle**, in
  [`configuration.md`](../../../docs/kernel/configuration.md): *no config
  option affects the problem's search lattice; config may only affect
  search speed*, in the user's words. A budget may stop the walk, and then
  the read-out says the answer is partial. Every flag row's *does it change
  the answer* column is re-read against the principle. A flag that does
  change it is named, and S1e2.1a.6 turns each one into a register row or
  shows it is a budget.
- `doc_audit.py` clean. The page stays in the *current* state, with no
  banner.
