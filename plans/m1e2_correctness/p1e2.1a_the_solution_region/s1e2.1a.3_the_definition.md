### The rulings came first (2026-10-16)

The user answered the sub-questions before the census was taken
([Q-M1e2.7 § The rulings, and the questions back](../open_questions.md#the-rulings-and-the-questions-back-2026-10-16)).
They ruled four sub-questions and one starting point:

- **the starting point:** in general R is its full node set, simplified only
  where the case allows or a query asks;
- **(a):** `k` = |R|;
- **(b):** `ein solve` studies the region first, and `hrules` move out of
  `(query …)`;
- **(c):** three forms, `(solve …)`, `(solution …)`, and `(query … :expect …)`
  over the goal's projection;
- **(i):** a component is a set of models. `Ambiguity` and `Contradiction`
  judge a stated expectation, and without one the read-out explores.

The user's second answer of the same day
([Q-M1e2.7 § The second answer](../open_questions.md#the-second-answer-2026-10-16))
settled the rest for the **monotone** case, which is this phase's:

- `k` is replaced by **k_s** and **k_c**;
- (f), (g) and (h) hold as answered for a monotone program;
- the user's grouping is named **projection classes**;
- ∅ is a solution exactly when root is alive and discharged, whatever the
  generator proposes;
- `solve`'s default is the full region, which is
  [S1e2.1a.9](s1e2.1a.9_solve_describes_the_region.md).

**W11 is not this stage's question.** It is revisited after the refactoring.

## Acceptance

- **The census**, `utils/region_census.py`: every corpus entry and register
  witness that declares a `solve` run, through `--dump-region`. It reports:
  - **n**: how many entries the oracle reaches at the default limit, and how
    many need it raised;
  - **|R|**, the size of each line, and whether the lines meet;
  - **holes**: which entries have them, and through which `absent`;
  - **the trivial line**: the obligations-only programs whose maximal line is
    *everything*;
  - **components and sub-cubes**: how many components, how many are single
    points (the well-posed case), and the dimensions;
  - **the duality**: where it holds, and whether it ever fails without a
    hole;
  - **commitments against states**: where two commitments give one state;
  - **the universe**: where the config-free candidate list differs from
    `alive₀`;
  - **monotonicity**: which entries S1e2.1a.1's detector flags, and on how
    many of them the cube observes a violation. Those entries are P1e2.1b's,
    and every number above is reported for the monotone ones separately;
  - **(a)'s unit**: |R| in commitments and in states, ∅ ∈ R, and the regions
    with one maximal node but several states. A first, unbanked take on
    2026-10-16 found 14, 107 and 35 of 154
    ([Q-M1e2.7](../open_questions.md#measured-for-these-answers)).

  It is written as a dated page beside the census pages M1d banked, and it is
  re-takable.
- **The user's rulings** on (a)–(i) except (d), written into Q-M1e2.7 and
  dated. Five of them are in as of 2026-10-16; the rest are listed above. (d) moved to [P1e2.1b](../p1e2.1b_the_non_monotone_lattice/README.md)
  on 2026-10-15. The
  stage puts the census on the table and a recommendation under each
  sub-question, and records the rulings. It argues only where a ruling would
  contradict a measured fact.
- **[`solution_semantics.md`](../../../docs/kernel/inference/solution_semantics.md)
  rewritten around R:**
  - the node classification, from [`definitions.md`](definitions.md);
  - R in general as its node set; the two lines, which are exact only when R
    is convex; holes; the ladder from S1e2.1b.6;
  - what k_s, k_c (ruling (a), (e)) and `exhausted` (g) mean, and the verdict
    words as judgements of a stated expectation (i);
  - ∅'s rule, and **complete** (*the rung proposes nothing*) replaced by
    **discharged**;
  - **root is `sat(program)` and is never written by the search** (proposed,
    third answer), and **a name is any constant the program mentions, rules
    included** (ruled, third answer);
  - states and their generators, as (e) is ruled;
  - empty R: no alive node, or every alive node owes. Which word each kind
    gets is S1e2.1a.5's, under (i);
  - the universe, defined without config, as (h) is ruled.

  The worked example (`lattice/02`) is redone as a drawing from S1e2.1a.2.
  § 6, *where the engine differs*, is rewritten against the new definition.
  [Q-M1e.6](../../../docs/history/m1e_review_processing/open_questions.md#q-m1e6--what-is-a-solution-and-what-is-a-model)
  gets a one-line forward pointer to Q-M1e2.7, and nothing else in M1e's
  record changes.
- **The config principle**, in
  [`configuration.md`](../../../docs/kernel/configuration.md): *no config
  option affects the problem's search lattice; config may only affect
  search speed*, in the user's words. A budget may stop the walk, and then
  the read-out says the answer is partial. Every flag row's *does it change
  the answer* column is re-read against the principle. A flag that does
  change it is named, and S1e2.1a.6 turns each one into a register row or
  shows it is a budget.
