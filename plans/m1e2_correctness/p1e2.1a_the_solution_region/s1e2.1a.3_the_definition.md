### The rulings came first (2026-10-16)

  `(query …)`;
- **(c):** three forms, `(solve …)`, `(solution …)`, and `(query … :expect …)`
  over the goal's projection;

## Acceptance

- **The census**, `utils/region_census.py`: every corpus entry and register
  witness that declares a `solve` run, through `--dump-region`. It reports:
  - **n**: how many entries the oracle reaches at the default limit, and how
    many need it raised;
  - **|R|**, the size of each line, and whether the lines meet;
  - **holes**: which entries have them, and through which `absent`;
  - **the trivial line**: the obligations-only programs whose maximal line is
    *everything*;
  - **components and sub-cubes**: how many components, how many are single
    points (the well-posed case), and the dimensions;
  - **the duality**: where it holds, and whether it ever fails without a
    hole;
  - **commitments against states**: where two commitments give one state;
  - **the universe**: where the config-free candidate list differs from
    `alive₀`;
  - **monotonicity**: which entries S1e2.1a.1's detector flags, and on how
    many of them the cube observes a violation. Those entries are P1e2.1b's,
    and every number above is reported for the monotone ones separately;
  - **(a)'s unit**: |R| in commitments and in states, ∅ ∈ R, and the regions
    with one maximal node but several states. A first, unbanked take on
    2026-10-16 found 14, 107 and 35 of 154

## Outcome (2026-10-16)

**The census.** `utils/region_census.py --all --levers`, 249 programs, 191 s on
12 jobs, run twice with identical tables. R is computed on 159 programs
(n ≤ 16); 35 are over the limit and 55 do not load. 93 of the 146 monotone
cubes have no candidate, and every table gives them their own column. What it
found:

- **The static check's theorem holds on every monotone cube**: no hole, no
  violation of upward closure, a duality that gives both lines. Holes are on 5
  of the 13 flagged programs, all of them known.
- **Ruling (f) holds** on every case the corpus has: 25 of 25 and 5 of 5.
- **∅ ∈ R on 41 of the 51** non-empty monotone regions with a candidate, and
  every exception owes at root. **k_s = 1 on 12 of them**, and 25 have one
  maximal node and several states.
- **k_c ≠ k_s on 13 regions**, up to 32 768 against 728 (`features/02`).
- **alive₀ ≠ H on 29 programs**: 7 lose ∅ to a forced positive, 26 drop a
  candidate before the search, and every dropped candidate is a dead
  singleton in the cube (66 of 66).
- **No lever changes R on 158 of the 159, and 23 of the 24 levers change it
  on none.** `enable-symmetric-mirror` does, on `features/06`: off, it takes
  three facts out of root. `-y` aborts one non-monotone program
  (`d3-unsat-core`) with *"Saturation commutativity violated"*.

## Decisions taken without a ruling

Each can be overruled here.

1. **The page reports `--all`.** The acceptance's selection, corpus entries
   that declare `solve` plus register files, is the census's default and is
   reported beside it. `--all` adds the 29 entries that declare no `solve`,
   reached at `-m 1`, because they hold the largest regions in the corpus.
2. **A cube with no candidate gets its own column.** 93 of 146 monotone cubes
   are one node, and counting them with the rest made ∅ ∈ R read 100 of 110
   where the programs with a lattice give 41 of 51.
3. **The lever comparison includes the states.** Status and class alone said
   *same* for `enable-symmetric-mirror` on `features/06`, whose only node is
   ∅ and whose model loses three facts. The comparison now reads root's
   fixpoint (`ein saturate --dump`) and every state in R
   (`--region-states`).
4. **Route 1's border is N.** Route 1 generates a node only when every
   one-smaller subset is alive, so its dead border is the minimal dead sets:
   18.4 % of the monotone cubes, where counting every dead node with an alive
   parent gives 24.3 %.
5. **The universe stays the generator's list.** (h) was ruled for the
   monotone case as *the universe is the candidate list*; the page names a
   declarative universe as what a check of the generator would be held to,
   and does not define one.
6. **Root is never written: still proposed.** The page says so in § 1.3, and
   S1e2.1a.9's acceptance assumes it.
7. **The drawings are today's renderer's.** The page draws `lattice/02` with
   `ein render region` as it ships, says what its MISSING and SPURIOUS labels
   compare, and leaves the wording to S1e2.1a.7.
