## Context

The verdict block prints `solutions (k)` and one block per recorded node.
`--json-summary` carries `verdict.k`, `solutions`, `open_states` and
`exhausted`. None of them names a line of R, so a reader cannot tell whether
`k = 2` counts minimal or maximal nodes. Once R is the answer, a number
that does not say which line it counts is a new instance of the defect this
milestone is about.

### The rulings (2026-10-16)

[Q-M1e2.7](../open_questions.md#the-rulings-and-the-questions-back-2026-10-16):

- **(a), (e): `k` is replaced by k_s and k_c**, \|R\| in states and in
  commitments, both printed (the second answer).
- **(i): `Ambiguity` judges a stated expectation of exactly one solution, and
  `Contradiction` an expectation of some.** Without an expectation the
  read-out **describes R**: it is exploring. Which of the questions in
  [§ What `solve` can answer](../open_questions.md#what-solve-can-answer-about-r-2026-10-16)
  it answers by default is this stage's proposal.

### The switch (ruling 4, 2026-10-16)

[`solve_flags.md` § 9](solve_flags.md#9-what-needs-the-users-ruling)'s ruling
4 puts the default's switch in this stage's commit, once, so that no corpus
golden moves twice. The approved interface
([`solve_interface.md` § 6](solve_interface.md#6-what-moves)) says what moves
with it:

- bare `ein solve FILE` describes R;
- § 2.1's nine options and § 2.4's nine config fields retire;
- the last CLI path to the walk goes;
- `ein oracle` gets its other five options (`-T`, `--json-summary`,
  `--models`, `-p`, `-P`);
- the interim layout the region modes print is replaced by this stage's
  read-out.

The register cannot reproduce the walk's wrong answers after that commit, so
its rows flip in the same commit, against the `right` blocks
[S1e2.1a.10](s1e2.1a.10_the_register_under_r.md) restated.

## Acceptance

- **k_s and k_c replace `k`** on every surface. Where the run cannot know R,
  because a budget stopped it or no route reaches the program, each is a lower
  bound and says so.
- **The default read-out describes R**: |R|, the two lines and their sizes,
  components, sub-cube dimensions, holes and monotonicity. The region itself
  is printed as `(region (model …) …)` with `(merged (to from) …)`: every
  state, and for each state with several generating commitments its minimal
  ones (the third answer). That is the form S1e2.1a.4 reads back. The verdict words
  are printed only against an expectation the program states.
- **Every surface says what it counts and which nodes it lists**: all of R,
  the minimal line or the maximal line. That covers the verdict block,
  `--json-summary`, `--dump-answer` (`models/` per line, named in
  `index.json`), `--models list|key|grid`, `--trace`'s headline and `ein
  test`'s report.
- **Empty R is split** into its two kinds: no alive node, and alive nodes that
  all owe. The 2026-08-25 ruling named them `Contradiction` and `Open`. Under
  (i) a word judges an expectation, so the exploring read-out describes each
  kind and names neither; the words are `--single`'s alone (ruled
  2026-10-16, [`read_out.md` § 11](read_out.md#11-the-users-rulings-2026-10-16)).
- **Holes** are reported through S1e2.1a.1's stub, under *assumes a monotone
  lattice*, until P1e2.1b's S1e2.1b.3 rules on (d). They are never silent.
- **`exhausted`** certifies what (g) says it certifies, and the read-out
  sentence says so.
- **A run past every route's reach is partial** (C3): it prints what it has
  certified and bounds for the rest, names the route that declined and why,
  and runs no walk.
- **The switch, in one commit**, with each item named in this file before it
  moves:
  - the corpus cells of `solve_interface.md` § 6:
    - 94 `solve -e` cells dropped;
    - 10 `--dump-region` cells to `ein oracle --dump`;
    - 8 `-m`, 8 `--dump-states` and 1 `-E` cell, each re-pointed or removed;
    - 12 `levers` columns, of which `-o score-sum` goes;
  - the six corpus files that set a retired config field;
  - the option census in `configuration.md` § 3, `help_surface.rs`,
    `help_shape.txt` and `config_reference.rs`;
  - every `:expect` that held only because the walk stopped early.
- **The register flips.** The 46 register runs are re-pointed to the mode that
  asks each row's question, `--maximal` for a model-set row. The 15 rows whose
  wrong answer is the walk's flip to `fixed`, and so do the rows S1e2.1a.10
  filed. Each one flips because its run now answers the restated `right`, a
  check that S1e2.1a.10's test already passes. A row that does not answer
  `right` after the switch stays `open`, and the reason is written here. Any
  `:expect` that stated a walk answer moves in the same commit.
- **Every golden that moves is named first**: the corpus summaries, the trace
  goldens, `read_out.rs`'s cells, `help_shape.txt`, `corpus_shapes.md5` and the
  register. An unexplained move is a stop.
