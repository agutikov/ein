## The method

This file states it. The stage performs it, on the register and the stage
files as they stand at P1e2.1c's close, when S1e2.1c.1–.4 have built the
default.

1. **Re-take the input.** `ein-cli/tests/wrong_answers.rs` green at that
   commit, which re-runs every row's `[case.now]` and holds an `open` row to
   its wrong answer and a `fixed` one to its right one. Any row that moved
   since 2026-11-02 is listed with the commit that moved it.
2. **Each open row, three questions**, each answered by a named check and not
   by reading:
   - (i) **Is `[case.now]` the row's `right`?** The register test says.
   - (ii) **Where does the mechanism live?** In code no route reaches; in what
     R shares with the oracle, the candidate list, the saturator or the core
     ([`solution_semantics.md` § 6.6](../../../docs/kernel/inference/solution_semantics.md#66-what-the-oracle-does-not-check));
     or in a language ruling being applied (W3's refusal, W8's node). The
     row's witness, run under `--events`, names the code path.
   - (iii) **Is the reading contested?** The row's `class` says. A contested
     row keeps its stage and its question, and this stage rules on none of
     them.

   A row **closes by construction** when (i) is yes and (ii) is code no route
   reaches. It flips to `fixed` with the test that holds it, in the commit
   that records the re-scope. Every other row survives into its stage.
3. **Each P1e2.2 stage**, one of four outcomes, with its reason and its new
   estimate:
   - **keep**: its rows survive and its scope is unchanged;
   - **shrink**: part of its scope named a mechanism that no longer exists,
     such as S1e2.2.5's three learning mechanisms under *respect*, or
     S1e2.2.6's check *at record*. What is left is written down;
   - **restate**: a P1e2.1b or P1e2.4 ruling changed its right answer or its
     question;
   - **drop**: every row it owns is `fixed`, and the code its fix was to
     change is deleted in step 5. The rows keep their `fixed` until the
     revisit reads them. The stage file stays, with a status line naming this
     stage and the reason, until the milestone closes.
4. **Recorded:** P1e2.2's README, its stage table, its estimate and its
   § Dependencies inside the phase; the milestone README's estimate paragraph
   and Phases row; each row's `owner` where a stage dropped; and S1e2.1.7's
   list of what the revisit reads.
5. **The walk's library.** What S1e2.1c.4 measured as reused
   ([T1e2.1c.4.4](s1e2.1c.4_the_flags_at_scale.md#task-t1e21c44--what-of-the-walks-library-the-routes-reuse),
   2026-11-03: `oblgen.rs`'s projection and witness scan, the ladder, and
   `SolveError`; nothing of the walk's tree) moves to the
   routes. The rest is deleted, and so are the tests that run it, each named
   before it goes: `solve_shape` and the shape digests it feeds
   (`ein-render/tests/golden/corpus_shapes.md5`), and the walk calls in
   `ein-infer/tests/interning.rs` and `refutation_under_absent.rs`. The stub's
   dead code goes with it if
   [S1e2.1b.5](../p1e2.1b_non_monotone_programs/s1e2.1b.5_the_detector.md)
   left it. So do the walk-only `EIN_*` names, with `configuration.md`'s census.
   Two shipped surfaces still call the walk's generator, and neither may break
   silently: `summary::root_block` (`hypgen::generate`, surface S4, question 1
   below) and `--json-summary`'s `leftover` block under `EIN_LEFTOVER`
   (`hypgen::generate_blind`). Each is kept, re-pointed, or left to its
   surface's stage, and named before the deletion.
6. **The levers.** Each unread flag either has a meaning on a route S1e2.1c.2–.4
   built, measured and held by `lever_invariance.rs`, or is retired, refused
   with the stage and the reason, as S1e2.1a.5 retired nine options. Which one
   is the user's (below). `configuration.md`, `help_shape.txt`,
   `config_reference.rs` and `help_surface.rs` move with it, named first.
