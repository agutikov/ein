## Context

The user, 2026-10-16: *"the default simple `solve` without flags we get
exhausive certifying full region description"*. S1e2.1a.9 gives that wherever
it is cheap: route 1 on a monotone program within a budget of 2¹⁹ facts beyond
root, and the cube at n ≤ 16. Past both, `ein solve` prints what it certified
and bounds for the rest (C3).

**The first answer** is
[`route1_reach.md`](../p1e2.1a_the_solution_region/route1_reach.md), taken
2026-10-16. At 2²⁰ visits on 12 jobs route 1 finished `zebra.ein`,
`zebra2.ein` and `zebra2-obligations.ein`
([§ 6](../p1e2.1a_the_solution_region/route1_reach.md#6-where-route-1-declines-and-zebras-region-counted)):
`zebra.ein` has 270 454 alive commitments, and its R is one state of 261 982
commitments with 63 minimal generators, in 25.2 s.
[`walk_and_region.md` § 6](../p1e2.1a_the_solution_region/walk_and_region.md#6-what-this-means-for-question-3)
counted 27 alive states against those 270 454 commitments. That gap is why
the state route exists.

## Acceptance

- **Route 1 past its default budget**, on the twelve and on every monotone
  slow entry not among them: per layer, the commitments visited, dead, owing
  and in R, **and the distinct states** among them; facts beyond root; wall at
  a stated `--jobs`; and peak RSS, taken as
  [S1e2.4.2](../p1e2.4_what_the_switch_left/s1e2.4.2_models_key_declines.md#how-it-is-measured)
  took it, under a cgroup cap. The ceiling (a budget and a wall) is stated
  with the command. A program that does not finish under it is reported with
  its last complete layer and the growth of its last layers, labelled as
  such. **No extrapolation is printed as a count.**
- **The everything-alive cubes, decided.** On a monotone program, ⊤ alive
  makes every node alive (L1), and ∅ discharged makes every node discharged
  (L2), so two saturations decide that R is the whole cube and k_c = 2ⁿ. That
  is [`read_out.md` § 6.2](../p1e2.1a_the_solution_region/read_out.md#62-a-partial-run-c3)'s
  X11 argument, here applied to all seven and checked on each. k_s needs the
  states: `square-bwd/floors.ein` has 27 559
  ([`solve_interface.md` § 3.4](../p1e2.1a_the_solution_region/solve_interface.md#34-what-it-costs)),
  and the stage counts the other six where the count finishes.
- **What a description costs as the alive set grows.** One table over every
  program above and every monotone program route 1 finishes at the default:
  n, |alive|, alive states, k_c, k_s, the two lines' sizes, the minimal
  generators, and describe's saturations, facts and wall. It is the table
  S1e2.1c.2 and S1e2.1c.4 are measured against, and it replaces
  `walk_and_region.md` § 6's *upper bounds* with counts where they finish.
- **The flagged programs past the limit**, listed with n and what route 2
  certifies today, beside P1e2.1b's warning. Their alive set is not measured:
  on them a node's state is open (F21's D2), and the search past the limit is
  F21's D7. Their row says so.
- **The page:** `alive_set_reach.md`, in this phase's directory, dated, with
  every command. `route1_reach.md` is P1e2.1a's record and is not edited.
- **No answer moves.** The stage measures.

## How it is measured

```sh
cargo run --release -p ein-infer --example route1_reach -- --budget <B> --jobs <J> FILE…   # per program, the ceiling stated
```

`route1_reach` gains a per-layer count of distinct states, read off the state
class each visited point already carries (`downset`'s `class`, which route 1
counts k_s by), so states and commitments come from one run. `--budget` is in
visited commitments and `--facts` in facts beyond root, the ruled unit. Peak RSS is `wait4`'s `ru_maxrss`, as S1e2.4.2's `peak.py` reads it,
under `systemd-run --user --scope -p MemoryMax=…`. The release build is
`./build.sh`'s, the machine and its state are `utils/bench_env.sh`'s, and the
commit and the date go on the page.
