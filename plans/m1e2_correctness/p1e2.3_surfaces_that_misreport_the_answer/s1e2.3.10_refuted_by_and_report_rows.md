## Why

**N7: `refuted by` names the wrong rule.** `refuted by` and `refuted_by` name the primary rule of the first contradiction found, which need not be a rule the core's derivation uses. [`alt-cap-core.ein`](../../../examples/ein-bugs/alt-cap-core.ein) and `alt-cap-core-reordered.ein` both print the core `(w1 X) (w2 X)`. One says `refuted by narrow` and the other `refuted by wide`, though only `wide` derives `(false)` from that core. Re-taken at `cdcc3994`, 2026-11-05. Parked in [S1e2.2.11 § Notes](../p1e2.2_wrong_answers/s1e2.2.11_alt_cap_core.md#notes) and [S1e2.2.12 § Notes](../p1e2.2_wrong_answers/s1e2.2.12_core_id_order.md#notes).

**N8: the test report drops `outside` and `types`.** `ein test --json-report` rows carry `region.monotone` and `status`, but no `outside` and no `types`, which the text surface prints beside the claim. The re-take was `ein test tests/region/07_cube.ein --json-report R -v` at `cdcc3994`: stdout prints `outside the universe: is-a …`, and the row's `region` is `{route, monotone, k_s, k_c, ms, status, stale_nodes, paths_differ}`. The omission was S1e2.2.8's default, and [S1e2.2.8](../p1e2.2_wrong_answers/s1e2.2.8_uncovered_certified.md) records it.

No answer moves; two surfaces say less than, or other than, what holds.

## The method

1. **Re-take first**, at the stage's HEAD, and list every golden either fix moves.
2. **N7.** A dead node's `refuted by` names the rule or rules whose derivation of `(false)` the printed core was taken over. Where two derivations give cores of the same size, it names the one the core is. The default is one rule per printed core, on every surface that prints `refuted_by`.
3. **N8.** The report's row carries `outside` and `types` as the summary does, with the same field names. The schema moves from `ein-test-report/3` to `/4`, additively.
4. **Tests:** both `alt-cap-core` files name `wide`. `tests/region/07`'s row carries `outside`.
5. **The pages:**
   - `read_out.md` (`refuted by`);
   - `answer_dump.md`'s `nogoods.json` row;
   - `configuration.md`'s `--json-report` row;
   - this phase's README: N7 and N8 fixed.
