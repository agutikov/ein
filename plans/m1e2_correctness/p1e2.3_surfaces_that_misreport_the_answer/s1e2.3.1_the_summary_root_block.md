### What `root_block` calls today

[`summary::root_block`](../../../ein.rs/crates/ein-cli/src/summary.rs) is
called by two functions, `build_region` and `build_monotone`. It forks the KB
it is handed. That KB is root's fixpoint already: every writer reaches the
summary through `region::prepare_with`, which saturates root in place
(argued, from the code). On the fork it:

1. runs `closed::emit_closed`, which writes `(__closed__ R)` for every declared
   relation no rule concludes. No route runs this pass;
2. saturates again, with a fresh `Saturator`. On a fixpoint this adds only
   what step 1's markers derive (one `closed-here` each on
   `tests/stdlib/closure/02` and `03`). `root.saturator`'s `naf_*` counters
   are this pass's;
3. runs the walk's `hypgen::generate`. It reads `enable-pre-branch-lookahead`
   and `enable-lookahead-kill-cache` off `SolverConfig`, where since S1e2.1c.5
   both sit at their defaults, **on**, on every program. It reads
   `EIN_OBLIGATION_CHOICE` through `oblgen::Choice::from_env`. Each lookahead
   kill writes `(not h)` into the fork;
4. counts the fork's facts after all three, and compiles the plans again for
   `root.plans`.

H is built once per run, elsewhere.
[`region::prepare_with`](../../../ein.rs/crates/ein-infer/src/region.rs) runs
`hypgen::generate_config_free` on root's fixpoint, without step 1. That pass
pins the lookahead and the kill cache off and the rung order to `rule-order`.
It then sorts the candidates and drops duplicates. Every route starts from
that root and that H.

### What went with the retirement, and what remains

**Gone.** Two disagreements went:

- the block moving with a program's lookahead or kill-cache flag: the loader
  refuses both flags;
- `-L` and `-K` beside a `config` echo: both are refused, and the echo no
  longer has those keys.

Nothing a CLI flag or a `(config …)` can set moves the block now.

**Remaining:**

- `root.facts` is not root's fixpoint on 181 of 218. The extra facts are
  `emit_closed`'s markers, what saturation derives from them, and the kill
  cache's writes.
- `root.hypgen` is not H on 71 of 218, in both directions. It describes the
  walk's generator, run with the lookahead on, over a KB that holds the
  markers.
- `EIN_OBLIGATION_CHOICE=off` moves the block on 32 files. `configuration.md`
  is wrong about it in three places, all for T1e2.3.1.4 to fix:
  - it says the variable *"moves `root.hypgen` and nothing else a run

    of the config levers only;
  - its § 2.3 also says *"root's fixpoint holds 23"*, where it holds 22.

  `lever_invariance.rs`'s module note also still says 23.
- **New with the retirement:** the block runs two filters, at *on*, that the
