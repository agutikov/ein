### Q-M1e.17: two of its three options are gone, and the clamp moved to `-g`

[Q-M1e.17](../../../docs/history/m1e_review_processing/open_questions.md#q-m1e17--three-int_arg-options-silently-reinterpret-a-negative)
found `-m` and `-E` clamping a negative to 0, and `-E`'s abort line printing
the clamped number. It offered three rulings: refuse below zero, honour the
negative, or refuse it and keep the clamp for library callers.

Every count option on the three commands that take one, at `-3` and at `0`,
on `examples/branching/05_mini_zebra.ein`, re-taken 2026-11-02:

| option | `0` | `-3` | held by |
|---|---|---|---|
| `solve -n` | refused, exit 2 | refused, exit 2 | `cli_semantics.rs` (`-n=0`, `--solutions=-1`, `-7`) |
| `solve -m`, `-E`; `test -m`, `-E` | retired: refused, exit 2, naming the replacement | the same | `region_modes::the_retired_options_are_refused_with_what_replaced_them`, `test_cli.rs` |
| `solve`/`test --region-budget` | refused: *"expected 1 or more facts"* | refused | `region_modes.rs` for `0`; nothing feeds a negative |
| `solve`/`oracle --region-limit` | accepted | refused: *"expected 0 to 63 candidates"* | nothing feeds a negative |
| `solve`/`oracle -j` | refused | refused | nothing feeds a negative |
| `solve`/`oracle -T` | aborts at once, exit 2, *"--max-time 0 s reached"* | aborts at once, exit 2, printing `-3` as typed | nothing |
| **`solve -g`** | **logs every node, as `-g 1`** | **logs every node, as `-g 1`** | nothing |
| `saturate --max-steps` | the limit is hit at once | honoured, printed as typed: `max_steps=-3` | Q-M1e.17's precedent |
| `saturate --progress-every` | disables, as its help says | disables | `help_shape.txt` |

So the false number is gone with `-E`. `-T` honours a negative the way
`--max-steps` does, and its message is true. Every new count option refuses
what it cannot mean. **What is left is `-g`**: `modes.rs:641` is
`.max(1)`, so `-v -g=0` and `-v -g=-3` print 14 node lines on `branching/05`,
as `-g 1` does, with no word. Its help says *"log every N-th node (default
100; set 1 to log every node)"* and says nothing of 0. The same option name on
`ein saturate` means *disable* at 0. This is the survey's S5, moved.

## Ruling

**Ruled 2026-11-03** (the user, by AskUserQuestion, 2026-11-03, on this file's question with the witness re-checked that day at `318e23b9`):

- **Q4, Q-M1e.5: (a).** *Experimental* is no licence to ship a false surface: the ruling of 2026-10-16 (no two commands may disagree, and every answer agrees with the oracle) answers it. Close Q-M1e.5 in M1e's register, citing that ruling, and hand the learning half to M1f's S1f.10.7.
- **Q5, Q-M1e.17 on `-g`: (b).** `-g 0` means no node lines, as `ein saturate --progress-every 0` does. A negative is refused (exit 2, a stderr `error:`). On 2026-11-03 `-g -1` was a parse error, while `-g=-1` and `-g 0` were accepted and silently clamped.
- **Q6, S7 and S8: (a), pin both.** S7's `d3-goal-row-order.ein` goes into the per-commit id-order sweep, and S8's `probes/nested-core.ein` gets a read-out check on its core's spelling, each as § The questions' table says. The question as put named the two witnesses the wrong way round (S7 as the nested core and S8 as the goal-row order). The ruling, *pin both*, does not depend on which is which.
