# Q-M1f.577 — `-P` leaves out the `open` facts as it leaves out the `(not …)`

`-P`, a state's positive facts, leaves out its `open` facts as it leaves out its `(not …)` facts, and so does its
form under `--dump-answer`.

**Why.** A view of a state's model alone is wanted beside the state whole.

**Held by.** `ein-cli`'s `factdump`; [`08-read-out.md`](../../ein/user/04-reference/08-read-out.md).
