# Q-M1f.60 — A fact of a relation nobody declares has no statement

A relation no `(relation …)` declares, made for an open-world head, has no form to carry a `:why`, and no other form
templates it. Its facts read as every fact does: one a rule derived by its reason, the rule's `:why`, one the program
states by its `:source` or as written. Declaring the relation gives its facts no text either: a declaration's `:why`
reads the declaration alone ([Q-M1f.58](Q-M1f.58-a-fact-reads-in-english-in-the-trace.md)).

**Why.** A `:why` is the text of the form it is written on; a declaration-less template would be a language form of
its own, written on nothing.

**Held by.** `ein_render::sentence`'s `statement`, which reads a declaration's `:why` on the declaration and nothing
else; [14-natural-language.md](../../ein/user/03-syntax-and-semantics/14-natural-language.md#3-a-fact-in-english-the-fallback-ladder) § 3 and § 5.
