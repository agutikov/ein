# Q-M1f.60 — A fact of a relation nobody declares has no statement

A relation no `(relation …)` declares, made for an open-world head, has no form to carry a `:why`, and no other form
templates it. A fact of one that a rule derived reads by its reason, the rule's `:why`; one the program states falls
back to its s-expression. A program that wants such a fact read declares the relation.

**Why.** A declaration is where a relation's text lives; a second, declaration-less template would be a language
form of its own for what a declaration already does.

**Held by.** `ein_render::sentence`'s `statement`, which reads a declared relation's `:why` and nothing else;
[09_natural_language.md](../../kernel/ir/03-ein-lang/09_natural_language.md) § 3 and § 5.
