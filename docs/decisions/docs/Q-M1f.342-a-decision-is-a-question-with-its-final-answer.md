# Q-M1f.342 — A decision is a question with its final answer, kept under its id

A decision is a question about Ein that has an answer, and the project keeps it as that answer alone: what holds
today, why, and what holds it. It keeps the id it was asked under, which is its name and points at nothing:
`Q-M1e2.24` names one decision whether or not anything else of its series exists. Only the answer that stands has a
file. A question answered more than once keeps its last answer; one whose answer another decision carries has no
file of its own, and a citation of it names the decision that carries the answer. An answer taken by default and an
answer ruled are alike, and every decision may be reopened: a reopened decision is a question again until it has an
answer, and then its file states the new one.

**Why.** The documentation states the current state
([Q-M1f.341](Q-M1f.341-the-project-documents-only-its-current-state.md)); the options a question weighed, the answers
it had before, who answered and when, are the history of the work. A stable id lets a page or a comment cite a
decision in one token, and keeps meaning the same thing after the work that asked it is gone.

**Held by.** [`docs/decisions/README.md`](../README.md), which states the format, and every file under
`docs/decisions/`, one a decision, its id its name.
