# Q-M1f.470 — Only a knowledge base is drawn in the canonical form

The pages draw in the canonical form only a knowledge base's objects and facts: a knowledge base, a rule's patterns,
a derivation's facts. Every other drawing, a region, a lattice, a cube, the hypothesis space, a state or a
construction, a dependency graph, a schematic or a flowchart, the design's diagrams, is drawn in whatever way shows
its point best, its nodes labelled with what they stand for
([Q-M1f.430](../cli/Q-M1f.430-the-canonical-form-draws-a-knowledge-base.md)).

**Why.** The canonical form is the graph a knowledge base is; a state, a set or a step is no such graph, and drawn
as one it would bury its own structure among its facts' arrows. A small lattice over a small knowledge base may draw
each state with its facts where that is the page's point, as an exception the page says.

**Held by.** The pages and their `diagrams/` folders; the region, lattice and h-space views the region and lattice
drawings copy ([Q-M1f.430](../cli/Q-M1f.430-the-canonical-form-draws-a-knowledge-base.md)).
