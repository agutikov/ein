# Q-M1f.469 — A derivation's drawing on a page marks its terminals with a double border and its core in red

A page's drawing of a derivation marks a terminal, a given fact or a commitment that a walk over the derivation
grounds out on, with a double border, and the core's facts in red. No octagon is filled.

**Why.** A fill is how a frame's absence would have to be read, and a drawing of facts fills nothing
([Q-M1f.398](Q-M1f.398-a-fact-in-a-context-only-is-drawn-in-a-frame.md)): a border and a colour mark a fact without
changing what kind of object it looks like.

**Held by.** The drawings of [cores](../../ein/reasoning/02-kb-evolution/09-cores.md), which say what each mark means.
