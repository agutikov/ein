# Q-M1f.700 — A cut of a knowledge base's drawing keeps the `open` facts about what it draws

A page's cut of what `ein render kb` draws ([Q-M1f.468](Q-M1f.468-a-pages-drawing-of-a-knowledge-base-may-be-a-cut.md))
keeps every `open` fact root holds whose arrow `2` is at something the cut draws: a fact it keeps, held or denied, a
relation's box, or a fact of the relation it is cut to, drawn in the frame `open ctx` where root does not hold it.
A cut that only leaves out a kind of fact, the declarations' or the activators', keeps every `open` fact. The page
says where it cuts that it keeps them, and shows them as Ein. A whole picture is what the command draws, root's
`open` facts among them.

**Why.** A drawing of root draws the `open` facts root holds, what it owes and its hypotheses
([Q-M1f.572](../read-out/Q-M1f.572-a-states-open-facts-are-part-of-it-listed-drawn.md)), and a cut is a part of
that drawing: an `open` fact about what the cut draws belongs to the part, and one about what it leaves out does not.
A cut that kept none would show root without what it supposes and owes; one that kept all would bury the section's
point among hypotheses of relations it does not draw.

that point at what it draws, and the reasoning pages
[`01-firing.md`](../../ein/reasoning/02-kb-evolution/01-firing.md),
[`02-saturation.md`](../../ein/reasoning/02-kb-evolution/02-saturation.md) and

fact.
