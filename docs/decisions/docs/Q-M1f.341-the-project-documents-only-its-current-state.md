# Q-M1f.341 — The project documents only its current state

The project's documentation, its pages, the comments of its code and of its `.ein` files, its manifest's notes and
its decisions, states what is true of Ein now. How Ein came to be so is not documented in the project: no page
records a step of the work, the date of an event, or a measurement of an engine that no longer exists, and no page is
kept as a superseded record under a banner. A page that stops describing Ein is rewritten or deleted. The history of
the work is the version control's, every change with its message.

**Why.** A record of the work reads only while the work it points at exists, and it describes Ein as an intermediate
state of its own development rather than as what it is. A reader who needs the history has it, whole, in the
version control; a reader of the documentation needs the system it describes. What the project decided stays, as
the decisions' final answers ([Q-M1f.342](Q-M1f.342-a-decision-is-a-question-with-its-final-answer.md)).

**Held by.** [`docs/decisions/README.md`](../README.md), which keeps the decisions as the current state;
[Q-M1f.343](Q-M1f.343-a-page-or-a-comment-cites-a-decision-by-its-id.md) and
[Q-M1f.345](Q-M1f.345-nothing-in-the-project-reads-links-or-names.md), which say what a page may cite.
