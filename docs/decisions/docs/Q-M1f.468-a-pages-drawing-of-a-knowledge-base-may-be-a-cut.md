# Q-M1f.468 — A page's drawing of a knowledge base may be a cut of the view

A drawing of a knowledge base on a page may be a cut of the view the command draws: the facts of the relations the
section is about, every fact they point at with them, and a framed fact only where a kept fact points at it. The
page says what the cut leaves out, the declarations' facts, the facts that apply a rule, the stored negations. A
whole view is kept as the command wrote it.

**Why.** A view draws every fact the knowledge base holds, and a section is about a few of them: a drawing of the
whole would bury its point among the declarations' and the activators' facts. Said beside the drawing, the cut
misleads no one about what the view draws.

**Held by.** The pages that show a cut, each saying what it leaves out beside its drawing; the decision that a
drawing of the engine's is kept as the file the command wrote
([Q-M1f.368](Q-M1f.368-a-diagram-is-a-file-and-a-page-shows-its-picture.md)) reads of a whole view.
