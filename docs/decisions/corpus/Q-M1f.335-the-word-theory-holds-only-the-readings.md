# Q-M1f.335 — The word theory holds only the readings its problems need

The word theory holds the readings of a word that its problems use: *has*, *gave*, *together* of two people,
*more*, *holds*, *of*, *shared* and the two readings of *need*. It does not hold *gave all* or *gave half*, *together*
over everyone in a problem, *how many times more*, sizes and part-filled containers, or fractions of a share.

**Why.** A word has many readings, each depending on the context, and the theory does not enumerate them: a reading
is added with the problem that needs it, where a claim can hold it.

**Held by.** `examples/word_problems/words.ein`, whose rules are the readings it holds, and the problems of the
directory, whose claims use every one of them.
