# Q-M1e2.3 — R is over the universe, and the read-out names what it leaves out

R is over H, the candidate list the generation ladder builds at root, and `exhausted` certifies all of R over H
and nothing beyond it. Where the rung that built H proposes no fact of a relation the program can conclude or ask
about, and the program does not scope that relation out itself, every surface says so: an `outside` row under
`universe` on every question, *no candidate: … R holds of it only what the program states and saturation
derives*, `root.hypgen.outside` in the summary, a line beside each claim `ein test` prints, and `ein oracle`'s dump.

**Why.** A certificate over a universe is true of that universe; read as a statement about the whole program it
would claim what no node tested. Naming the relations left out keeps the certificate's scope on the page beside it.

one witness per rung and a program that leaves nothing out. The universe:
