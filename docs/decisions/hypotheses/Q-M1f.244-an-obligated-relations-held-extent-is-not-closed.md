# Q-M1f.244 — An obligated relation's held extent is not closed, whatever generates

An obligation that owes a fact of a relation, and a `(__closed__ R)` of the same relation, written or derived, are a
conflict, refused at load whatever else writes H, beside a rule that concludes a hypothesis too: root holds `(open R)`, and a closure,
as the two lists, configures the blind enumerator and may not disagree with the `open` facts root holds
([Q-M1f.578](Q-M1f.578-the-two-lists-and-a-closure-configure-the-blind.md)). `:hypothesis-relations` leaving out, or
`:no-hypothesis` naming, a relation an obligation owes is refused the same way. The refusal is one line naming the
`open` fact root holds, the obligation that writes it, the closure and where each comes from.

**Why.** A relation is closed when no commitment extends its held extent, and an obligation is paid only by a fact
of the relation it owes: a state that owes it could never be paid.
